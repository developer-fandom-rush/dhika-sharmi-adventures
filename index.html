FWFS-E00 · Opening story draft

Episode 00 — The Courtyard, Saturday Morning

Review draft · Not locked canon

One connected prologue for four married friends; no four repetitive introductions. The ending leaves story directions open.

Author's note

NOTE: Four Walls, Four Secrets is original fiction. All fictional characters, conversations and situations belong to the new world. DHIKA is used only to study narrative structure.

DISCLAIMER: Any romantic or intimate storylines concern adults aged 18 or older; consent and boundaries are meaningful. Children appear only in ordinary family and community scenes.

Noida ke The Courtyard society mein Saturday ki subah kuch zyada hi halchal thi. Central lawn mein decorators fairy lights laga rahe the. Community hall ke bahar chairs ki unloading ho rahi thi, aur society ke WhatsApp groups mein Anika ki Sunday wedding celebration se zyada Saturday rehearsal ki discussions chal rahi thi.

Aarohi Bansal apne dining table par laptop kholkar ek client ke design revisions check kar rahi thi.

Rey: Mumma, mera blue wala dinosaur kahan hai?

Aarohi: Kal tumne sofa ke neeche rakha tha, champ.

Kitchen se Ritvik ki awaaz aayi.

Ritvik: Aur kal se tumhara dinosaur wahin incident resolution ka wait kar raha hai.

Aarohi hans padi.

Aarohi: Tum apne production incidents office tak hi rakho, please.

Ritvik ne coffee ka cup uske saamne rakha. Pichhli raat deployment emergency ki wajah se woh der se soya tha, lekin subah Rey ke saath time nikalne ka usne promise kiya tha.

Phone vibrate hua: Four Seasons — 14 unread messages.

Kiara (group): Ladies! Saturday rehearsal ke liye outfits FINAL kar lo. Last-minute drama nahi chalega.

Zoya (group): Kiara, last-minute drama tumhari personality ka permanent feature hai.

Mira (group): Can we first confirm the rehearsal timing? Kal teen different messages aaye the.

Aarohi (group): Good morning, girls. Main 11 baje free ho jaungi.

Kiara (group): Aarohi madam, aap 11 baje free nahi hongi. Aapko 10:30 baje mere boutique mein hona hai.

Aarohi ne apna phone Ritvik ko dikhaya.

Ritvik: Tumhari friends se negotiation karne ka courage mere paas nahi hai.

Aarohi: Aur production incidents handle kar lete ho?

Ritvik: Servers predictable hain. Kiara nahi.

Society ke doosre tower mein Mira Sinha breakfast ke saath apne accounts review kar rahi thi. Aditya newspaper padh raha tha.

Aditya: Tum Saturday ko bhi balance sheet dekhogi?

Mira: Bas ek client reconciliation hai.

Aditya: Tumhare 'bas ek' ka matlab minimum do ghante hota hai.

Mira ne muskurakar laptop band kar diya.

Mira: Fine. Breakfast pehle.

Dono ke beech ki comfortable khamoshi unki shaadi ka ek purana, familiar hissa thi.

Kiara Mehta ke ghar mein situation bilkul alag thi. Mahi living room mein dance practice kar rahi thi, Samar hospital se aaye ek call ko finish kar raha tha, aur Kiara ek saath teen outfit covers check kar rahi thi.

Kiara: Samar! Tumne Mahi ki rehearsal shoes dekhi hain?

Samar: Shoe rack mein.

Kiara: Wah. Aaj doctor sahib ne ghar ki koi cheez locate kar li.

Mahendra Mehta balcony se newspaper fold karte hue andar aaye.

Mahendra: Main Mahi ko rehearsal ke liye le jaa sakta hoon.

Kiara: Thank you, Papa. Aap hi mere ghar ke sabse dependable aadmi hain.

Samar: Objection.

Mahi: Overruled, Papa!

Poora living room hans pada.

Zoya Arora apne bedroom mirror ke saamne earrings select kar rahi thi.

Dev: Tum wedding mein jaa rahi ho ya wedding ki marketing campaign launch kar rahi ho?

Zoya: Dono mein presentation matter karti hai, Mr. Arora.

Dev: Phir meri presentation approve hai?

Zoya ne uski shirt dekhi.

Zoya: Change it.

Dev: Expected.

Kuch hi der baad Four Seasons group par ek naya message aaya.

Kiara (group): 11 AM. Boutique. No excuses.

Mira (group): I thought we agreed on 11:30.

Zoya (group): Mira, tumhari problem hai tum Kiara ki baaton ko legally binding samajhti ho.

Aarohi (group): See you all soon ❤️

The Courtyard mein chaaron families apni-apni Saturday routine mein busy thi. Unki dosti purani thi, marriages established thi, aur zindagiyan ek doosre se judi hui thi. Magar har ghar ki apni ek private duniya bhi thi — jiska poora pata kabhi-kabhi sabse kareebi doston ko bhi nahi hota.
