Dear Readers,

Welcome to Four Walls, Four Secrets — the riveting journey of four married friends whose everyday lives hide four very different private worlds. Ankita is quiet with strangers but warm and playful with those she trusts. Shivani guards her feelings, Shreya wears her confidence openly, and Tanya notices what others leave unsaid.

Nothing important happens overnight. Every small moment matters, and every secret brings a new question.
