## Episode 02 · Ankita · Part 5 — Ek Pal Ki Ghalti
**Status:** READER DRAFT · NOT CANON LOCKED
**Continuity:** Thursday, immediately after Ankita E02 Part 4 EPP home-office walkthrough (~4:40 PM). Ankita still wears her cream-green saree and coral blouse. Ritvik is in his separate work call. Rey's afternoon care arrangements remain unaffected.
**DHIKA structural lens:** E02 L110–135, errand → practical entry → bag/wardrobe accident → two distinct witnesses → early exit → aftermath. This FWFS version starts at the already-established Bansal home visit; it does not pretend Karan and Aman need to enter again.

### Thursday · 4:52 PM · Bansal Apartment

001 “Monday se hamara new chapter start,” Karan ne kaha, EPP ka notebook band karte hue.
002 Ankita ne aankhen sikodi. “Chapter nahi, project. Aur Monday ko sab time par aana.”
003 Aman ne desk ke paas rakha tablet uthaya. “Ek chhota issue reh gaya hai. Prototype test phone ka connector main abhi check kar leta hoon?”
004 “Sure,” Ankita ne kaha. “Grey pouch mein adapter hai. Main device files nikaal deti hoon.”
005 Ritvik apni meeting join karke andar ke workroom mein ja chuka tha. Living room mein sirf teenon aur laptop fan ki halki awaaz thi.
006 Karan ne room ko dekha. “Honestly, Ankita, tumne home office kaafi thoughtfully design kiya hai.”
007 Ankita hansi. “Karan, aaj compliments ka quota exceed nahi ho gaya?”
008 “Professional appreciation. Main invoice bhi bhej sakta hoon.”
009 Aman muskuraya, lekin uski nazar test phone par thi. “USB-C adapter mil gaya. Bas cable verify karni hai.”
010 Ankita ne apna cream-green saree ka pallu kandhe par seedha kiya aur side table par rakha file bag uthaya.
011 Bag mein test cards, notebook aur chhote cables the. Ek strap thoda dheela tha, jise Ankita ne subah se theek nahi kiya tha.
012 “Yeh folder,” Ankita ne kaha, “Monday se pehle read kar lena. Sabse important failed-state screens marked hain.”
013 Karan uski taraf muda. “Yes, boss.”
014 Ankita ne bag table par rakhna chaha, lekin ek file corner se nikal kar neeche phisalne lagi.
015 Reflex mein usne file pakadne ko haath badhaya. Bag ka doosra strap uske pallu ki edge mein ulajh gaya.
016 Agle hi pal bag neeche gira aur pallu kandhe se khinchkar aage sarak gaya.
017 Uske coral blouse ka upar ka hissa pal bhar ke liye uski marzi se zyada khul gaya. Ankita ko pehle sirf girte samaan ki fikr thi.
018 Woh file uthane ko aur jhuki, tabhi usne mehsoos kiya ki saree apni jagah par nahi hai.
019 “Oh—!” Uski awaaz ruk gayi. Usne seedha hokar turant pallu theek kiya.
020 Karan ka agla mazaak aadhe shabd par tham gaya. Woh ek pal ke liye startled tha, phir apni nazar files par le aaya.
021 Aman ne turant chehra doosri taraf kar liya. “Main... cable rakh deta hoon,” usne dheere se kaha.
022 Ankita ka chehra garam ho gaya. Use apne haathon ki movement tak awkward lag rahi thi.
023 “Sorry. Bas... ek minute,” usne kaha.
024 “It's okay,” Karan ne bina koi joke kiye jawab diya. “Tum comfortable ho jao. Hum yahan rakh dete hain.”
025 Ankita ne fallen file ko table par rakha, pallu fir se kandhe par pin kiya aur bedroom ki taraf chali gayi.
026 Darwaaza band hote hi woh mirror ke saamne ruk gayi.
027 “Pehle file kyun pakdi? Pallu kyun nahi?” usne khud se poochha.
028 Tuesday ko Chotu ke saamne jo moment tha, woh uska socha-samjha decision tha. Aaj yeh ek unfortunate accident tha.
029 Yeh fark use sabse zyada disturb kar raha tha: Karan aur Aman uske professional collaborators the, aur Monday ko wapas aane wale the.
030 Woh unke reactions ko lekar sure nahi thi. Usne na koi remark suna tha na koi aisi baat jo misunderstanding clear kar sake.
031 Bahar living room mein Aman ne Karan se kaha, “Connector working hai. Main updated build remote se bhej dunga.”
032 Karan ne samjha ki is waqt zyada rukna inappropriate hoga. “Haan. Let's leave. Ankita ko message kar dete hain.”
033 Aman ne apna tablet case band kiya aur Ritvik ke workroom ke darwaaze ki taraf bina jaaye main door ke paas aa gaya.
034 Karan ne normal awaaz mein kaha, “Ankita, hum nikal rahe hain. EPP ka Monday slot confirmed hai. Notes mail kar denge.”
035 Ankita ne bedroom se jawab diya, “Okay, thanks. Sorry, thoda clumsy ho gaya.”
036 “No problem at all,” Karan ne kaha. Is baar uski awaaz mein koi teasing nahi thi.
037 Aman ne sirf “Bye, Ankita” kaha aur corridor mein nikal gaya.
038 Karan ne fallen cable pouch table par theek rakha, lights off kiye bina door khola aur uske peeche chala gaya.
039 Darwaaza band hua toh Ankita kuch der aur bedroom mein khadi rahi.
040 Usne socha Aman ne itni jaldi kyun exit kiya. Sharm se? Respect se? Ya uncomfortable hone ki wajah se?
041 Karan jo hamesha bolta rehta tha, woh bhi achanak itna quiet kyun ho gaya?
042 In sawaalon ke jawab ke liye abhi uske paas koi evidence nahi tha.
043 Kuch minute baad Ritvik apni meeting se nikla. “Guests chale gaye?”
044 “Haan,” Ankita ne normal hone ki koshish karte hue kaha. “Monday se sessions start.”
045 “Great,” Ritvik bola. “Setup theek laga unko?”
046 “Haan. Bas ek chhota mishap ho gaya. Main dinner ke baad bataungi.”
047 Ritvik ne uska expression dekha. “You okay?”
048 “Haan. Mujhe abhi bas thoda space chahiye.”
049 “Okay,” usne kaha. “Main Rey ko check karke aata hoon.”
050 Ankita ne phone uthaya. Screen par Karan ka short message tha: *Thanks for showing us the setup. Monday 3 PM confirmed. Aman will send the build.*
051 Koi personal remark nahi tha. Phir bhi message padte hi woh ek saath relieved aur embarrassed hui.
052 Usne likha: *Thanks. Updated test checklist kal bhej dungi.*
053 Reply send karke usne pending household errands ki list kholi. Society store se kuch cheezein chahiye thin.
054 Usne store par phone kiya aur Chotu ka naam liya. “Chirag available hai kya? Ek delivery aur purane verification ka status poochhna tha.”
055 Store par baithi aunty ne kaha, “Aaj woh regular shift par nahi hai, beta. Uske kuch personal kaam hain. Koi aur packet bhej sakti hoon.”
056 “Theek hai, abhi order hold kar dijiye,” Ankita ne kaha. “Kal bata dungi.”
057 Phone rakhte hi Tuesday ka Chotu phir yaad aaya—uska “Bye didi” aur tez kadam. Kya uski absence ka us din se koi sambandh tha? Ankita nahi jaanti thi.
058 Tabhi Rey ghar aaya aur apna rocket uske paas laakar bola, “Mumma, new launch ready!”
059 Ankita ne use gale laga liya. “Captain, pehle homework. Phir Mars mission.”
060 Rey ki hansi ne uska dhyaan kuch der ke liye badal diya. Lekin raat tak Karan aur Aman ka awkward exit uske mann mein rehne wala tha.

**Part 5 ends — Thursday evening.**
