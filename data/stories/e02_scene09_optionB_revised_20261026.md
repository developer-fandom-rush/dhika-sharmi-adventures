Continuity: after final Scene 8 Shreya sees Ritvik at the dining-side counter. Scene 3 established mutual recognition. Option B uses Shreya's deliberate, brief hand-on-hand touch by the water jug, Ritvik's chosen reciprocation, and Samar's interruption. The touching is not a definitive promise or diagnosis of any physiological reaction.

S9-L001. Flashback ke baad present mein, Ritvik dining-side counter par water jug rakh raha tha. Samar kitchen mein dinner ke baad ka kaam dekh raha tha, aur baaki family ki baatein dining table se aa rahi thi. Rey aur Mahi drawing room mein the. Shreya ko Scene 3 ki baat yaad thi; usne khud jug lene ke bahaane Ritvik ke paas jaana choose kiya.

S9-L002. Mahendra ke room mein kuch der pehle jo dekha tha, uski yaad Shreya ko ab bhi uneasy kar rahi thi. Lekin Ritvik ke saamne khade hote hue woh ek baat alag samajh rahi thi: Mahendra ka chhupkar kiya gaya behaviour aur Ritvik ke saath uski pehle se mutual, openly acknowledged attraction ek jaise nahi the. Scene 3 mein Shreya ne bhi conversation aage badhayi thi. Aaj woh usi confidence se dobara baat karegi, bina Ritvik ki marzi assume kiye.

S9-L003. Shreya jug ke paas ruki aur uski aankhon mein dekh kar boli, “Aaj pehle kitchen mein tumhari nazar… woh ordinary nahi thi.” Usne sawaal kisi mazaak ke parde mein nahi chhupaya; woh jaanna chahti thi ki Ritvik ab bhi apni pehli baat par khada hai ya nahi.

S9-L004. Ritvik ka haath jug ke handle par tha. Usne apni nazar uthayi aur kuch pal Shreya ko dekha. “Tumne notice kar liya tha,” usne pehle ki tarah dheemi awaaz mein kaha. Is baar woh turant topic badalne ki koshish nahi kar raha tha.

S9-L005. Shreya ke chehre par chhoti si muskurahat aayi. “Sirf notice nahi kiya tha. Maine bhi toh kitchen mein baat jaan-bujhkar aage badhayi thi.” Yeh kehkar usne ek pal ruk kar uska expression padha; phir jug lene ko haath badhaya.

S9-L006. Ritvik jug ko stable rakhne ke liye handle pakde raha. Shreya itni paas thi ki unki baat doosre logon tak nahi pahunch sakti thi. Usne dekha ki Ritvik chup hai, lekin apni position se hata nahi. Shreya ne apna agla kadam jaldi mein nahi liya.

S9-L007. Jug uthate waqt Shreya ne apni ungliyan jaan-bujhkar Ritvik ke haath par rakh di. Do chhote seconds tak usne haath nahi hataya. Ritvik ne apna haath kheenchne ke bajaye uski taraf dekha aur halki si smile di. Shreya ko samajh aa gaya ki touch accidental nahi tha aur Ritvik ne use notice kiya hai.

S9-L008. Shreya ne pehle apna haath hataaya, phir jug ki taraf dekha. “Tumhe bhi farak pad raha hai… is baat ka,” usne halki awaaz mein kaha. Woh jawab sunna chahti thi, lekin use Ritvik ke mann ki har baat pehle se maloom hone ka daawa nahi tha.

S9-L009. Ritvik ne dining table ki taraf ek nazar daali aur gehri saans li. “Zyada mat padho isme, Shreya,” usne kaha. Uske lafzon mein sakhti se zyada ehtiyaat thi; ghar mein sab log abhi paas hi the.

S9-L010. Shreya ek pal ko ruki aur dheere se boli, “Theek hai. Main kuch assume nahi karungi. Tum uncomfortable ho toh main yahin ruk jaati hoon.” Usne jug sambhaal liya aur Ritvik ko poori jagah di ki woh chahe toh baat wahin khatam kar de.

S9-L011. Ritvik ne khud usse dobara dekha. “Aisa nahi hai. Bas sab log yahin hain.” Shreya ne halki si muskurahat ke saath poochha, “Ab bhi soch rahe ho ki kehna chahiye ya nahi?” Ritvik ke honthon par bhi muskurahat aa gayi. “Ab toh tum khud pooch rahi ho.” Dono samajh rahe the ki yeh Scene 3 wali baat ka agla kadam tha, koi galatfehmi nahi.

S9-L012. Usi waqt kitchen se Samar ki awaaz aayi. Shreya ne jug utha kar ek kadam peeche liya. Ritvik ne counter se haath hata liya aur normal tone mein poochha, “Aur kuch chahiye?” Shreya ne sir hila diya. Dono apne-apne kaam ki taraf mud gaye; kisi aur ko unki private baat ka andaaza nahi hua.

S9-L013. Door jaate waqt Shreya ko Ritvik ki dheemi awaaz, uska haath na hataana aur “Bas sab log yahin hain” kehna yaad raha. Woh in gestures ko kisi future promise mein nahi badal rahi thi, lekin usse pata tha ki unki pehli baat sirf uski imagination nahi thi. Mahendra ki disturbing discovery uske mind mein ek alag secret thi; Ritvik ke saath is deliberate moment ki memory bhi ab uske paas thi. Uski kuch curiosity abhi bhi unresolved thi.
