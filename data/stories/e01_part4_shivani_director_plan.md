## Agreed chronological beats
1. In the office parking area, Shivani discovers that her scooty genuinely will not start. It is a real setback, unlike Bantu's fabricated petrol excuse in the comparison source. Raghav and Rishabh see her trying to start it, check the situation, and offer help.
2. She hesitates about accepting a motorcycle ride with three people because it is crowded and unsafe, but eventually accepts a short trip.
3. Seating is exact: Raghav drives, Shivani is between the brothers, and Rishabh sits at the back. Raghav sees an opportunity for a more memorable encounter; Rishabh does not know his brother's intentions.
4. The bike passes two or three speed breakers. Its movement forces uncomfortable proximity. Raghav's choices at the bumps are not purely accidental; the reader is made aware that he has an ulterior motive.
5. While maintaining balance, Rishabh holds on near Shivani's waist over the saree. A shift in seating brings the passengers closer than she expected. His quiet apology is heard close to her ear, and she notices his warm breath near her neck and shoulder.
6. Shivani's point of view: a sudden jolt, a bodily shiver, a brief pause in breathing, embarrassment, and uncertainty about what just happened. She cannot easily change her seating position while the bike is moving. Her outward reaction is restrained, but the event remains on her mind. Do not assume an involuntary sensation establishes willing participation.
7. Rishabh is privately conscious of his own reaction during the cramped ride; Shivani cannot be sure how to interpret every sensation or movement. Raghav's private motive is a separate secret. Neither brother openly explains everything to the other.
8. At her building, Shivani asks to stop, dismounts, straightens her saree and thanks them. The ride ends without a confession or romantic decision.
9. Afterwards the brothers talk briefly, showing outward casualness against their different concealed thoughts. Later, an apology text arrives about the awkward trip. Shivani replies that she appreciates their help. The reply shows provisional trust, not knowledge of Raghav's hidden motives.
