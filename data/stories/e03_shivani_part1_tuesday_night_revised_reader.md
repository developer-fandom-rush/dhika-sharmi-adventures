006 Dinner mein dono ne banking escalation, Shivani ke audit aur society ke wedding arrangements par baat ki. Aditya thaka tha, lekin Shivani ki baaton ko dhyaan se sun raha tha.
007 Baad mein living room mein woh TV ke saamne saath baithe. Kuch minute mein Aditya ka sir Shivani ke kandhe par tik gaya. “Tum itni jaldi so gaye?” Shivani ne dheere se poochha.
008 “Nahi. Bas tumhari shoulder service test kar raha hoon,” Aditya ne aankhen band rakhkar kaha. Shivani muskurayi aur TV ki awaaz kam kar di.
009 Shivani, thirty-three, profession mein measured aur evidence-driven thi. Personal life mein bhi woh jaldi conclusion nahi banati thi; lekin kisi apne ke saamne uski reserved smile aasani se shararat mein badal jaati thi.
010 Aditya, thirty-eight, banking ke demanding schedule ke bawajood uski independence ka respect karta tha. Uska humour quiet tha; Shivani ki tarah woh bhi har personal feeling sabke saamne announce nahi karta tha.
011 Shaadi ke shuruati dino mein Shivani affection express karte hue thodi hesitant thi. Aditya ne use kabhi hurry nahi ki. Saalon mein dono ne apni comfortable rhythm bana li thi—care, teasing, affection aur ek-doosre ke kaam ke liye jagah.
012 Shivani ko unke saath guzre saal yaad aaye—busy workdays, chhoti-chhoti nok-jhonk, aur woh familiarity jisme har baat explain karna nahi padta tha. Aditya uska trusted partner tha; uske paas baithna ab bhi ghar lautne jaisa lagta tha.
013 Aditya ne aankhen kholi. “Kal ka koi pending courier issue hai?” Shivani hansi. “Nahi. Bas ek blouse tailor ko bhejna hai. Dori toot gayi.”
014 “Wahi jiske baare mein Monday ko warn kiya tha?” Aditya ne poochha. Shivani ne use dekha. “Haan, auditor sahib. Tumhara observation correct nikla.”
015 “Main bank mein risk assess karta hoon,” Aditya bola. “Ghar mein bhi aadat reh gayi hai.” Shivani ne uske shoulder par halka sa punch maara. “Risk report kal dena. Ab so jao.”
016 Aditya ko dori tootne ka pata chal gaya, lekin Chirag ne use baandhne ki koshish ki thi aur dono kitne awkward hue—yeh usse maloom nahi tha. Shivani ko bhi samajh nahi aa raha tha ki woh yaad baar-baar kyun laut rahi hai.
017 Bedroom ki light band hui. Aditya ne uska haath thaama; Shivani uske paas aa kar let gayi. Kuch der mein woh so gaya, lekin Shivani ki neend der se aayi.
018 Usne decide kiya ki Chotu ke liye course details kal dekh legi. Jo hua tha uske baad normal conversation karna hi shayad sabse achcha rahega. Is soch ke saath usne aankhen band ki.
019 *Wednesday, 7:50 AM.* Shivani subah uthkar routine kaam nipta chuki thi. Laptop par ek audit reconciliation file khuli thi; Aditya kitchen mein coffee bana raha tha.
020 “Aaj bhi numbers se race?” Aditya ne cup rakhte hue poochha. Shivani ne bina nazar uthaye kaha, “Numbers kam-se-kam apna reason toh dete hain.”
021 Aditya hansa. “Toh main meeting join kar loon. Jab tumhare numbers cooperate kar dein, breakfast kar lena.” Shivani ne file save ki aur cup utha liya.
022 Uski nazar ek pal course brochures par gayi. Tuesday ki chhoti si awkwardness phir yaad aayi, lekin usne abhi koi message nahi bheja. Bahar ek naya Wednesday shuru ho chuka tha.
