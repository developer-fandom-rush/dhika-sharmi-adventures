### ANKITA — Saturday, 6:15 PM · Bansal Apartment

Cab se ghar lautkar Ankita ne laptop bag sofa ke paas rakha. Studio ki testing khatam ho chuki thi, magar Karan ki khuli tareef aur Aman ka woh achanak use sambhaal lena abhi dimaag se poori tarah gaya nahi tha. Uski deep-teal fitted top par din bhar ki silvatein thi; ivory trousers ab bhi neat the. Loose hair ka ek curl gardan ke paas chipak raha tha. Cream stole usne chair par rakh diya.

“Mom, rocket ki lights nahi jal rahi!” Rey ne door ke paas se awaaz di.

“Rocket mein battery lagayi bhi thi, captain?” Ankita ne poochha.

“Battery toh khatam ho gayi.” Rey ne bilkul serious expression banaya.

Ankita hans padi. Ritvik ne drawing room se kaha, “Emergency supplies maine society store se mangwa di hain.”

Kuch der mein doorbell baji. Bahar familiar delivery boy khada tha—**Chirag**, jise sab **Chotu** kehte the. Baais saal ka, hamesha jaldi mein, lekin Rey ke liye chocolate le aana kabhi nahi bhoolta.

“Didi, rocket rescue package!” Chotu ne batteries aur stationery ka packet aage kiya.

“Tumhare bina humari space agency bandh ho jaati, Chotu Babu.”

“Phir salary increase bhi karwa do, didi.”

“Pehle launch successful karo.”

Chotu hanskar Rey ke paas baith gaya. Rey ne use rocket ki body dikhayi; Ritvik dusre room se toolbox lene chala gaya. Ankita coffee table ke doosri taraf jhukkar chhota wire uthane lagi. Us waqt cream stole chair par hi tha. Jhukne se top ka neckline thoda khula aur **cleavage ki halki jhalak** Chotu ki taraf ho gayi.

Chotu ne dekha aur agle hi pal nazar batteries par kar li. Uski chalti hui baat achanak ruk gayi.

“Red wire... woh... didi, tape milega?”

Ankita seedhi hui. Usne Chotu ki rukti awaaz aur turant jhuki aankhen dekhin; phir ek baar apni top ki taraf nazar gayi. Usne stole uthakar kandhe par rakha. Gaalo mein halki garmahat thi—na jaane Chotu ki jhijhak se, ya bas is ehsaas se ki woh khud achanak self-conscious ho gayi thi.

“Kya hua, Chotu? Rocket dekhkar nervous ho gaye?”

“Nahi didi. Bas wire chhota hai.”

“Accha. Toh engineer ko kaam karne do.”

Do minute baad rocket ki LED jal uthi. Rey khushi se uchhal pada. Chotu ne hamesha ki tarah usse high-five diya. Ankita ko uske normal ho jaane se rahat mili, lekin uska woh ek pal ka sannata yaad reh gaya.

Delivery ka bill dekar Chotu nikal gaya. Ghar phir roz jaisa lagne laga.

Tabhi laundry attendant ne pehle lautaya hua ek labelled packet gate par chhod diya. Ankita ne kapde ginne shuru kiye. Packet ka ek compartment khaali tha; **ek matching innerwear set missing tha**.

“Strange. Maine toh poora set bheja tha,” usne dheere se kaha.

Usne laundry service ko message draft kiya. Use ye nahi pata tha ki uske naam wala packet pehle galti se **Mahendra ke flat** pahunch chuka tha—aur Mahendra ne baaki packet lautate waqt woh set apne paas rakh liya tha. Chotu ka is raaz se koi lena-dena nahi tha.

Ankita ne light band karte hue din ki teen alag baatein yaad ki: Aman ki awkward closeness, Chotu ki nazar ka achanak hatna, aur packet se missing kapde. Kisi ko ek doosre se jodne ka uske paas abhi koi kaaran nahi tha.

### SHIVANI — Saturday, 7:05 PM · Sinha Apartment

Shaam tak Shivani ne apni sea-green saree theek se sambhaal li thi, lekin dopahar ki bike ride bhulaana aasaan nahi tha. Raghav ke jokes, Rishabh ki kaan ke kareeb ki apology aur speed breakers ke jhatke—sab kuch yaad aate hi woh pal bhar ko khamosh ho jaati.

Dining table par Monday ke accounting folders rakhe the. Printer labels aur kuch file clips ki delivery pending thi.

Doorbell par Chotu mila.

“Shivani didi, aaj toh bill se zyada files hain!”

“Monday ko audit hai, Chotu. Tumhara bhi kar doon?”

“Aap karogi toh meri toh ek bhi entry pass nahi hogi!”

Shivani ke chehre par halki muskaan aa gayi. Usne parcel andar rakhwaya aur receipt sign ki. Chotu ne seedhe tareeke se kaha, “Waise didi, aaj ki sea-green saree bahut achchi lag rahi hai. Earrings bhi.”

“Tum logon ne aaj compliments ki committee bana rakhi hai kya?” Shivani ne hansi dabate hue kaha.

“Kaun log?”

“Kuch nahi. File clips gine hain?”

Ek folder upper cabinet mein phansa tha. Shivani use neeche khinchne ke liye palti aur haath upar badhaya. Dopahar ki thakaan mein saree ka pallu pehle hi halka loose tha; iss harkat se woh kandhe se ek taraf sarak gaya aur blouse ki **khuli back aur bare upper-back skin** darwaaze ki taraf dikh gayi.

Chotu invoice pakde khada tha. Uski nazar ek second ke liye wahaan gayi; woh turant kagaz par dekhne laga.

Wall mirror mein Shivani ne uska badla hua expression pakad liya. Usne pallu kandhe par laakar pin ko seedha kiya.

“Invoice mein kuch galat hai kya?”

“Nahi didi, sab theek hai.”

“Phir itna serious kyon ho gaye?”

“Monday audit ka darr lag gaya,” Chotu ne kamzor sa joke kiya.

Is baar Shivani khulkar hans di. “Jaao, bach gaye. Aaj sirf delivery check karungi.”

Woh chala gaya, lekin Shivani ko aaj doosri baar apne kapdon aur kisi aur ki achanak chuppi ka khayal aaya. Usne khud ko samjhaya: *Har nazar ka matlab ek jaisa nahi hota.*

Kapde badalne ke liye bathroom mein gayi toh drying stand par latki **bra ka strap doosre hook par atka** nazar aaya. Aam taur par woh use bahar wale hook par rakhti thi. Usne hanger ko theek kiya aur samjhi ki shayad hawa ya ghar ke kisi kaam mein hil gaya hoga.

Reader ko pichhli baat maloom thi: **Prakash** isi ghar mein aakar us garment ko bina permission chhoo chuka tha aur baad mein jaldi se wapas latka gaya tha. Shivani ko us baat ka andaaza nahi tha.

Phone par Raghav-Rishabh ki taraf se ride ki apology ab bhi saved thi. Shivani ne Monday ki report ke reminder ke paas use rehne diya aur lamp off kar diya.

### SHREYA — Sunday, 3:10 PM · Boutique

Dopahar ka sound-check khatam hua toh Shreya seedhe boutique pahunchi. Plum boat-neck kurti, silver jhumkas aur half-tied hair mein woh din bhar kaam sambhaal rahi thi. Neel ka headset adjust karna aur Aarav ka guitar lesson kabhi-kabhi be-wajah yaad aa jaata, toh woh khud par hans padti.

“Madam, packing rolls aa gaye,” assistant ne kaha.

Saath mein Chotu bhi tha—brown-paper rolls, tape aur garment covers lekar.

“Arre, Chotu Babu! Aaj bhi express delivery?”

“Didi, aapka boutique hi sabse zyada express demand karta hai.”

“Good. Toh tumhe award dena padega.”

“Chocolate chalegi.”

“Woh toh Mahi ke liye laate ho. Apne liye kya loge?”

“Bas ek din late delivery par daant mat dena.”

Shreya zor se hans di. “Impossible promise.”

Assistant next counter par customer ki fitting lene chali gayi. Shreya ne Chotu se ek fabric roll pass karne ko kaha. Upar wale rack mein jagah banane ke liye woh step-stool par khadi hui. Uski **waist-length kurti** ki side thodi upar khisak gayi; **kamar ke kinare ki bare skin** dikh aayi.

Chotu ne roll upar karne ke liye nazar uthayi. Ek pal ko uska haath ruka, phir usne roll theek jagah par diya.

“Chotu? Roll pakde raho,” Shreya ne kaha.

“Ji, didi.”

Neeche utarte waqt Shreya ko side par hawa ka halka ehsaas hua. Usne kurti ka hem neeche kiya aur Chotu ki khamoshi notice ki.

“Kya hua? Bill bhool gaye ya boutique ka fashion sense samajh nahi aaya?”

Chotu sharmaakar hasa. “Aapke saamne kaun fashion discuss karega, didi?”

“Achha, ye toh smart answer tha.”

“Waise aaj earrings aur hairstyle bahut suit kar rahe hain.”

“Thank you! Tum toh Aarav se competition kar rahe ho aaj.”

“Aarav kaun?”

“Ek guitarist. Bahut bolta hai—tumse bhi zyada.”

Un dono ne phir normal billing ki baat ki. Shreya jaanti thi ki ab woh aasani se logon ka awkward silence pakad leti hai. Magar woh Chotu ki taraf waise attracted nahi hui jaise use sound-check par apne aap ko Neel aur Aarav ke kareeb mehsoos karke surprise hua tha. Yahaan use zyada curious amusement thi—aur ek halki jhijhak.

Shaam ko ghar pahunchkar Shreya ne kapde badalne ke liye wash area khola. **Black lace bra** hanger par thi, lekin uski position pehle jaisi nahi thi. Usne apni aadat ke mutabik dono straps ek hi direction mein rakhe the; ab ek palta hua tha.

“Main hi jaldi mein rakh gayi houngi,” usne socha, aur hanger seedha kar diya.

Reader ko yaad tha: **Harish** ne pehle ghar ke private wash area mein bina permission woh garment uthaya tha aur awaaz aane par wapas rakha tha. Shreya ko uska pata nahi tha.

Mahi ki awaaz aayi—“Mumma, song sunao!”

Shreya muskurakar bahar aayi. Guitar ki ek galat chord aur kisi ka playful compliment phir yaad aa gaya. Woh melody gungunaane lagi.

### TANIYA — Sunday, 12:50 PM · Arora Apartment

Storeroom mein subah teen mishaps hone ke baad Taniya ne khud ko finally sofa par bithaya. Maroon saree ka pallu usne phir se pin kiya tha; nape par paseene ki halki nami thi. Vihaan, Arjun, Dhruv aur Laksh abhi bhi wahi the. Chaaron ne use ghar tak chhod diya tha aur ab remaining game-props checklist verify kar rahe the.

“Bhabhi, aaj ka game ‘Survive the Storeroom’ rakhein?” Laksh ne poochha.

“Tumhare liye ‘Keep Quiet for Five Minutes’ zyada challenging hoga,” Taniya ne jawab diya.

Arjun ne paani ka glass diya. “Seriously, bhabhi. Aaj aapne jitna kaam kiya, kisi ko bhi thakaan ho jaaye. Phir bhi maroon saree mein full event-ready lag rahi ho.”

“Compliment note kar liya. Ab kaam par focus.”

Vihaan ne doorbell suni. Chotu cardboard carton aur registration slips lekar khada tha.

“Arey, Chotu!” Taniya ne kaha. “Perfect timing. Tumhari delivery hi reh gayi thi.”

“Bhabhi, mujhe sab log Chotu Babu bulate hain. Aap sirf Chotu kyon?”

“Kyunki tum mujhe ek aur nickname de doge toh main chaar devaron ke alawa tumhari bhi class lungi!”

Laksh turant bol pada, “Bhabhi, hum paanch ki team ho gayi?”

“Bilkul nahi. Tum chaar kaafi ho.”

Chotu ne carton neeche rakha. Taniya usi box par rakhe delivery register mein sign karne jhuki. Pallu ka kinaara side ko saraka aur saree ki pleats thodi hileen; **navel aur stomach ka chhota sa hissa** pal bhar ke liye nazar aaya.

Chotu ne register ki taraf dekhte hue woh badli drape notice ki, phir aankhen neeche kar li. Taniya ne pen rakhkar pallu sambhaala. Usne Chotu ki ek pal ki chuppi pakad li thi.

Laksh ne turant chheda, “Chotu bhai, signature ho gaya. Ab bhi bill padh rahe ho?”

“Payment check kar raha tha,” Chotu ne kaha.

“Tu khud register nahi dekh raha tha, Laksh,” Dhruv ne shaant lehje mein kaha.

Arjun hansa. “Bhabhi ke saamne sabka viva ho raha hai.”

Taniya ne aankhen badi ki. “Ek aur word bola na, toh saare cartons tum chaaron uthaaoge. Chotu ko main chai pilaungi.”

“Dekha!” Laksh ne kaha. “Usko chai aur humein duty!”

“Aur tumhe extra duty,” Taniya ne bina ruke jawab diya.

Sab hans diye. Vihaan ne quietly carton uthaya aur doorway clear kar diya. Usne apne saathiyon ki nazron ka direction notice kiya tha, magar Taniya ke saamne koi alag comment nahi kiya. Arjun ke liye uska confidence hi sabse appealing raha; Dhruv ne uski thakaan aur recovery observe ki; Laksh ko usse ek aur hansi nikaal lena jeet laga.

Chotu ne payment receipt di aur chala gaya. Taniya ne ek pal socha: *Pehle ye log mujhe chhedte the, main jawaab deti thi. Aaj sab meri aankhon ka reaction bhi padhne lage hain kya?* Usse apni pehle ki ghabraahat aur aaj ke compliments ka fark samajh aa raha tha.

Baad mein washroom shelf par **ek personal garment ulte fold mein** rakha dikha. Taniya ne use seedha kiya.

“Ye kisne is tarah fold kiya?” woh halki awaaz mein boli, phir socha shayad ghar ke kaam mein kapde ulat gaye honge.

Reader ko Part 3 se pata tha ki **Virendra** ne pehle us item ko bina permission chhua aur jaldi mein alag tarah fold karke rakh diya tha. Taniya ko iska koi andaaza nahi tha.

Living room se Laksh ki awaaz aayi: “Bhabhi, next game ki rehearsal cancel?”

Taniya ne muskurakar jawab diya, “Rehearsal cancel nahi. Bas tumhari comedy par fine lagega!”

### CLOSING CUT — CHOTU

Raat ko convenience store bandh karte waqt Chotu ne receipts sort ki. Bansal, Sinha, Mehta aur Arora—chaar alag ghar, chaar familiar women, aur do din mein kuch aise awkward pauses jo uske liye naye the.

Ankita ke confidence ke saamne woh apni zubaan kho baitha tha. Shivani ki sharmili muskaan aur phir invoice wala excuse use yaad tha. Shreya ke boutique mein hansi-mazaak ne tension turant halka kar diya. Taniya ke ghar mein toh chaar devaron ne uski ek pal ki chuppi bhi joke bana di.

Chotu ne receipts band kar di. Usse kisi ghar ke **missing ya displaced private garments** ke baare mein kuch nahi pata tha. Woh sirf apni deliveries jaanta tha; chaaron auraton ke alag andarooni sawaal nahi.

Aur chaaron gharon mein, din ki normal routines ke beech, ek-ek chhota sawaal bina jawab ke reh gaya.
