# FOUR WALLS, FOUR SECRETS  
## Episode 02 · Ankita · Part 1 — The Marriage Behind the Smile

*Tuesday evening · Bansal Apartment*

Shaam ke lagbhag 6:30 baj rahe the.

Ankita kitchen counter ke paas khadi thi. Soft-blue sleeveless kurti ab bhi usne pehni hui thi — soft fabric uske shoulders se smoothly gir raha tha, neckline simple thi, aur uske arms bilkul khule hue the. Coffee table ke documents woh kuch der pehle samet chuki thi, lekin laundry complaint ka folder ab bhi alag rakha tha.

Doorbell baji.

Rey apne rocket ke parts chhodkar seedha darwaaze ki taraf bhaaga.

“Papa!”

Ritvik ne darwaaza khola hi tha ki Rey uski taangon se lipat gaya.

“Arre, Captain! Landing permission toh le lo!”

“Papa, rocket ki light chal gayi!”

“Very good. Matlab aaj engineering department mein kisi ki naukri bach gayi.”

Ankita kitchen se hans padi.

“Tum dono ko engineering jokes se retirement kab milegi?”

Ritvik ne bag sofa ke paas rakha. Woh thaka hua dikh raha tha — shirt ke sleeves elbows tak folded the, shoulders thode droop kiye hue. Phir bhi jab usne Ankita ki taraf dekha toh uski aankhon mein wohi familiar half-smile aa gaya.

Woh Ankita se lagbhag paanch-chhe inch lamba tha. Average se thoda better build, arms aur shoulders mein clear muscle definition thi jo office ke baad bhi dikhai deti. Haath bade aur steady the. Face sharp tha, jawline clean, aur jab woh seriously sunta tha toh uski aankhen bilkul still ho jaati thin.

Usne Ankita ke kandhe par haath rakha. Haath garam tha, thoda heavy.

“Coffee milegi? Aaj teen production incidents solve kiye hain.”

“Ek minute. Pehle fresh ho jao. Coffee bhi milegi aur sympathy bhi.”

Ritvik bedroom ki taraf chala gaya. Rey uske peeche apne rocket ki poori technical explanation dete hue gaya.

Ankita ne unhe jaate dekha.

Kuch ghante pehle isi hall mein Chotu verification ke documents photograph kar raha tha. Woh kaam khatam karke itni jaldi nikla tha ki Ankita ab tak uske reaction ko poori tarah samajh nahi paayi thi.

Use yaad tha ki usne apni position jaanbujhkar nahi badli thi, balki Chotu ki reaction jaanne ke liye kuch der tak waise hi bani rahi thi.

Us waqt ki curiosity ab bhi thi. Uske apne decision ko lekar excitement bhi thi. Lekin Ritvik ki familiar awaaz sunkar ek naya sawaal saamne aaya — kya woh apne pati se aisi koi baat keh sakti thi?

Usne coffee mugs nikale aur apne aap ko filhaal koi jawaab dene se rok liya.

---

Ankita Bansal ab battis saal ki thi.

Freelance UX designer ke taur par woh clients, developers aur product managers ke saamne apni baat confidence se rakhti thi. Kisi design mein galti dikhe toh seedha challenge kar sakti thi. Lekin uska personal background us professional personality se kaafi alag tha.

Woh ek relatively conservative ghar mein badi hui thi.

Uske parents education aur career ko importance dete the, lekin relationships aur personal intimacy par khulkar baat nahi hoti thi. Ghar mein achchi parvarish ka matlab sirf achievements nahi, balki apne conduct aur reputation ka dhyan rakhna bhi tha.

Ankita ko college mein friends ke saath rehna, outings par jaana aur modern kapde pehenna pasand tha. Lekin parents ke saamne apni pasand openly express karne mein woh aksar hesitant rehti.

Romantic relationships ke baare mein bhi uski soch traditional thi. Woh shaadi se pehle kisi serious relationship mein nahi aayi thi. Physical closeness aur affection uske liye aisi cheezein thin jinhe woh marriage aur mutual trust ke saath jodkar dekhti thi.

Lekin iska matlab yeh nahi tha ki Ankita kamzor ya kisi aur ke decisions par depend karne wali ladki thi.

Career usne khud choose kiya tha. Job aur professional independence ko lekar parents se kai baar debate bhi ki thi. Bas personal feelings ko words dena uske liye professional disagreements se zyada difficult raha tha.

Ritvik se uski pehli mulaqat families ke introduction ke through hui thi.

Us waqt Ritvik tees saal ka tha aur engineering operations mein kaam karta tha. Technically strong, thoda sarcastic, aur itna relaxed ki pehli formal meeting mein bhi kisi awkward silence ko joke bana sakta tha. Uska posture seedha tha aur baat karte waqt uske haath natural rest position mein rehte — restless nahi.

Ankita ko yaad tha, unki pehli coffee meeting mein Ritvik ne kaha tha —

“Main ek warning pehle hi de deta hoon. Meri job mein kabhi-kabhi raat ko bhi production incidents aa jaate hain.”

Ankita ne bina soche poochha tha, “Aur tum personal life mein bhi har problem ko incident bolte ho?”

Ritvik hans pada.

“Depends. Agar tum gussa ho toh critical severity.”

“Achha. Aur resolution?”

“Pehle root cause analysis.”

Ankita ne pehli baar us meeting mein khulkar hansi thi.

Usse Ritvik ka confidence achcha laga tha, lekin usse zyada woh baat yaad rahi thi jo usne meeting ke end mein kahi.

“Ankita, marriage ke baad tum apna UX career continue karna chahti ho na? Mere liye woh bilkul normal hai. Tum apni job, clients aur decisions khud handle kar sakti ho.”

Yeh baat Ankita ke liye important thi.

Woh apni professional identity ko shaadi ke baad chhodna nahi chahti thi. Ritvik ne ise koi favour nahi, balki partnership ka ordinary part maana tha.

Dheere-dheere phone calls, coffee meetings aur family interactions ke through dono comfortable hue.

Ankita ko uska humour pasand tha. Ritvik ko Ankita ka thoughtful nature aur woh confidence pasand tha jo uske kaam ke baare mein baat karte hi uski awaaz mein aa jaata tha.

Kuch mahine baad dono ne shaadi kar li.

---

Shaadi ki pehli raat Ankita bahut nervous thi.

Do din ki ceremonies, relatives, photographs aur endless congratulations ke baad woh physically exhausted bhi thi.

Ritvik room mein aaya toh Ankita bed ke corner par seedhi baithi thi. Kandhe thode andar ki taraf the, haath godi mein rakhe hue, jaise kisi formal interview ka agla question aane wala ho. Uski saansen halki-halki tez thin.

Ritvik ne use dekha aur muskuraaya.

“Madam, interview finish ho chuka hai. Offer letter mil gaya.”

Ankita hansi, lekin uski nervousness poori tarah nahi gayi. Uske fingers thode tight the.

Ritvik uske paas baitha. Mattress uske weight se thoda dab gaya.

“Thak gayi ho?”

“Bahut.”

“Toh so jaate hain. Baaki duniya kal discuss kar lenge.”

Ankita ne use dekha.

“Bas?”

“Bas. Tum kya expect kar rahi thi?”

“Nothing,” usne jaldi se kaha. Awaaz mein halka sa crack tha.

Ritvik ne uske haath par apna haath rakha. Uska haath bada tha, garam, aur bilkul steady. Ankita ko laga jaise uski ungliyon aur haathon ka tension thoda kam ho gaya.

“Ankita, hum dono ke paas bahut time hai. Tumhe kisi cheez mein hurry karne ki zarurat nahi.”

Us raat Ritvik ne uske forehead par kiss kiya. Soft, der tak nahi — bas ek clear, calm contact. Phir usne halka sa khinchkar Ankita ko bed ke beech ki taraf le aaya. Ankita ne side se lette hue apni position adjust ki. Ritvik uske bagal mein stretch hokar let gaya. Ankita ki peeth uske seene se thodi door thi, lekin uska haath ab bhi uske haath par tha. Dono kuch der baat karte-karte so gaye.

Yeh incident Ankita ko saalon baad bhi yaad tha. Shaadi ki beginning mein uski shyness Ritvik ke liye frustration nahi bani thi.

Unki closeness dheere-dheere develop hui.

Pehle Ankita openly hug karne mein bhi hesitant hoti thi. Ritvik jab uski tareef karta, woh aksar nazar hata kar kisi practical baat par aa jaati.

Ritvik use tease karta, lekin agar woh genuinely uncomfortable hoti toh baat wahi rok deta.

Dono ke beech romantic aur physical intimacy mutual comfort ke saath badhi. Ankita apni preferences express karna dheere-dheere seekhi. Kuch cheezein jo pehle awkward lagti thin, waqt ke saath familiar ho gayin; kuch boundaries usne ab bhi rakhi hui thin, jinhe Ritvik respect karta tha.

Kapdon ko lekar bhi yahi difference tha.

Ritvik ko Ankita modern dresses aur sleeveless outfits mein achchi lagti thi — especially jab fabric uske shoulders aur arms ko clearly dikhata tha. Woh usse parties mein apni pasand ke kapde pehenne ke liye encourage karta, lekin Ankita kai outfits ghar par pehenna zyada comfortable samajhti thi.

Ritvik ko aksar uski yahi baat amusing lagti.

“Client meeting mein poori company se argue kar sakti ho, lekin sleeveless dress pehenkar restaurant jaane mein problem hai?”

Ankita ke paas usually ek ready answer hota.

“Client meeting mein log mere design ko judge karte hain.”

“Aur restaurant mein?”

“Wahan tum mujhe judge karte ho.”

“Main toh already five stars de chuka hoon.”

Ankita hanskar uske shoulder par halka sa punch maar deti. Uska shoulder solid tha; punch ka impact usse kuch nahi hota tha, lekin woh hamesha thoda exaggerate karke “ow” bolta tha.

Ritvik ko uski shyness pyari lagti thi, lekin woh chahta tha ki Ankita kisi bhi outfit ya personal decision ko sirf doosron ki judgement ki wajah se reject na kare.

Ankita ko bhi apne husband ke saamne khud ko express karna pehle se aasaan lagne laga tha.

Rey ke janam ke baad dono ki routine badal gayi.

Ritvik ke late-night incidents, Ankita ki client meetings, Rey ki schooling aur ghar ke everyday responsibilities ne unke personal time ko kam kar diya. Kabhi Ritvik ka urgent call dinner ke beech aa jaata, kabhi Ankita client revision mein busy rehti.

Lekin unhone apne relationship ko sirf responsibilities tak limited nahi hone diya.

Weekend coffee, chhoti arguments, unexpected compliments, ek-doosre ke workload ko samajhna — yeh sab unki married life ka normal hissa tha.

Ankita Ritvik se pyaar karti thi. Uska trust kisi ek romantic moment par nahi, balki saalon ke shared decisions par bana tha.

---

“Madam UX Designer?”

Ritvik ki awaaz ne Ankita ko present mein la diya.

Woh fresh hokar kitchen ke paas khada tha. Baal thode geele the, t-shirt simple thi. Thakan ab bhi thi, lekin chehra saaf tha.

“Coffee ban rahi hai ya main incident ticket raise kar doon?”

Ankita ne mug uski taraf badhaya.

“Ticket closed.”

Ritvik ne ek sip liya, phir uski taraf dekha. Uski nazar soft-blue kurti par thodi der ruki.

“Waise aaj tum achchi lag rahi ho. Ye blue wali kurti kaafi time baad pehni hai na?”

Ankita ek pal ke liye chup hui.

Ye kurti usne Monday shaam khud select ki thi. Tuesday afternoon mein Chotu ke aane par bhi wahi pehni thi. Fabric halka tha, aur jab woh move karti thi toh open arms ke around soft shift feel hota tha.

“Hmm. Comfortable hai,” usne kaha.

“Good choice.”

Ritvik ka compliment bilkul natural tha. Ankita ne smile karke coffee uthayi.

Lekin kuch seconds ke liye use Chotu ka abruptly nikal jaana phir yaad aaya.

Ritvik ne uski khamoshi notice ki. Woh ek kadam aage aaya aur uske bagal mein counter se lag kar khada ho gaya. Uska shoulder Ankita ke shoulder se halka sa touch ho raha tha — garam, solid, familiar.

“Everything okay?”

“Haan. Bas thoda long day tha.”

“Client problem?”

“Ek verification tha. Ab complete ho gaya.”

Ritvik ne sir hila diya.

“Good. Ab Rey ki engineering disaster bhi dekh lo.”

Living room se Rey ki awaaz aayi.

“Papa! Mera rocket disaster nahi hai!”

Dono hans pade.

Ritvik Rey ke paas chala gaya. Jaate-jaate usne Ankita ke upper arm par halka sa pinch kiya.

Ankita ne apna mug counter par rakha. Woh jaanti thi ki Ritvik ka sawaal genuine concern se aaya tha.

Ankita jaanti thi ki usne jaanbujhkar Chotu ki reaction dekhni chahi thi. Chotu ne use dekha tha aur bina baat badhaye jaldi chala gaya tha. Lekin Chotu uska irada samjha tha ya nahi, Ankita ko iska pata nahi tha. Ritvik ne woh encounter nahi dekha tha, aur Ankita ne use bataya bhi nahi tha.

Usne coffee ka ek aur sip liya aur kitchen counter ke against thoda sa lean kar liya. Soft-blue fabric uske skin ke against halka sa shift hua.

Bahar se Rey ki hansna aur Ritvik ki awaaz aa rahi thi.

Andar, Ankita ne apni aankhen band ki aur ek second ke liye bas khadi rahi.

Woh khud abhi apni feelings ko samajhne ki koshish kar rahi thi. Usse Chotu ki reaction ko lekar curiosity thi, aur apni intentional choice yaad karke excitement bhi. Ritvik ki familiar care ke saamne usne apne decision par dobara socha; saath hi guilt bhi mehsoos hua. Yeh alag-alag feelings thin, aur koi ek doosri ko automatically cancel nahi kar rahi thi.

Us shaam Ankita ne Rey ke rocket ki wiring check ki, Ritvik ke saath dinner kiya aur usual family conversation mein participate kiya.

Ritvik ko Tuesday afternoon ki personal incident ka pata nahi chala.

**Part 1 ends — Tuesday night.**
