# Four Walls, Four Secrets
## Episode 01 — Part 6: Chaar Faisle
*Reader-preview full prose · Monday afternoon to Tuesday early afternoon · original fiction · adult ensemble · not yet final canon*

### ANKITA — MONDAY, 4:10 PM · BANSAL APARTMENT

Ankita ne laptop band kiya toh screen par uska hi reflection reh gaya. Lunch ke baad se woh laundry service ke teen emails padh chuki thi. Teesra email bhi pehle do jaisa tha: *We are investigating your complaint.*

“Investigating,” usne dheere se dohraya. “Kapde missing hain, koi murder mystery nahi.”

Phone ka screen chamka. Chirag ka message tha: *Didi, app wala form submit ho gaya. Ek verification code ka doubt hai. Kal aa sakta hoon?*

Ankita ne turant reply type kiya—*Haan, screenshot bhej do.* Phir ungli ruk gayi. Kal saamne baithkar uska form samjhate waqt Chotu kitna dhyaan se sun raha tha. Aur usse ek din pehle, Rey ke rocket ke paas, baat karte-karte achanak chup kyun ho gaya tha?

Woh baat usne kisi se nahi kahi thi. Ritvik se bhi nahi. Isliye nahi ki koi bada raaz tha; bas isliye ki woh khud use ek naam nahi de pa rahi thi.

Almirah se kapde nikaalte waqt uski nazar soft blue sleeveless cotton kurti par padi. Summer mein woh ise ghar par pehenti thi. Pichhle kuch dino se usne zyada tar workwear hi pehna tha—tailored trousers, fitted tops aur woh cream stole jo har meeting mein sambhalna padta.

Usne kurti pehni, baal ek loose knot mein bandhe aur mirror ke saamne ruki. Neckline uski usual office kurti se thodi khuli thi; kapda halka tha, lekin comfortable. Woh apne aap ko dekhkar hansi.

“Ankita Bansal. Client calls mein five stakeholders sambhal leti ho. Ek delivery boy ki awkward silence analyse nahi kar paa rahi?”

Mirror ne jawaab nahi diya.

Phone par usne likha, *Kal 4 baje aana. Rey school se late aayega, aaraam se form dekh lenge.*

Send karne ke baad use khayal aaya ki usne “aaraam se” kyun likha. Form sach mein dekhna tha; yeh bhi sach tha ki woh Chotu ka pehle wala surprise dobara samajhna chahti thi.

**READER-ONLY — ANKITA:** Usne outfit sirf uske liye khareeda nahi tha, lekin aaj use chunne mein Chotu ki nazar ki yaad zaroor shamil thi. Yeh faisla uska apna tha. Use koi idea nahi tha ki Chotu kal ko uski kis baat par react karega.

Ritvik ka call aaya. “Evening ko jaldi aa paunga. Rey ke science project ka kya hua?”

“Scientist ne launch postpone kiya hai,” Ankita ne jawab diya. “Aur haan, laundry complaint abhi pending hai.”

“Main follow up kar deta hoon.”

“Nahi, main kar lungi.”

Usne phone rakha aur apne reflection ko ek baar phir dekha. Abki baar usne smile chhupaayi nahi.

### SHIVANI — MONDAY, 6:35 PM · SINHA APARTMENT

Shivani ke saamne Monday ka audit workbook khula tha, magar uski nazar baar-baar phone par chale jaati. Raghav aur Rishabh ka report-review theek raha tha. Kaam ke waqt kisi ne Saturday ki uncomfortable bike ride ka zikr nahi kiya. Dono poore din numbers aur proofs ki baat karte rahe.

Ghar aakar usne seedha Aditya ko message kiya: *Dinner late mat karna. Main half an hour mein free.*

Phir Chirag ki note aayi: *Didi, jo courses suggest kiye the, unka brochure print mil gaya. Aapko dikhaun?*

Shivani ne likha: *Kal shaam, saat baje. Aditya ki files bhi kal courier kar dena.*

Woh wardrobe ke paas gayi. Sea-green saree ab neatly folded thi. Uske bagal mein ek cream saree aur matching blouse rakha tha—back par dori baandhne wala, jise woh festivals mein pehenti thi. Pehle bhi is blouse ki ek dori dheeli hui thi; usne pin se manage kar liya tha aur tailor ko dena bhool gayi thi.

Shivani ne blouse uthaya. Back ka design kaafi khula tha, lekin front properly fastened tha. Usne use mirror ke saamne hold kiya. Hair bun karne par kandhon ke beech ki skin nazar aati. Pehle woh sochti thi ki us outfit par Aditya kya kahega. Aaj usse ek aur naam yaad aaya—Chotu.

Usne khud se poochha, *“Agar woh dekhe toh? Kya main itni sharmilaungi jitna kal hui thi?”*

Isi sawaal se use sabse zyada ghabrahat hui.

Woh Chotu ko ek mehnati, decent ladka samajhti thi. Kal breakfast par jab usne padhaai ki baat ki thi, Shivani uske liye genuinely khush hui thi. Aaj pehli baar woh notice kar rahi thi ki uski apni curiosity bhi is connection mein aa gayi hai. Yeh baat use achchi bhi lagi aur ajeeb bhi.

Aditya room mein aaya. “Woh blouse phir se? Tumhe yaad hai na iski dori pehle loose thi?”

“Haan. Tailor ko dena hai.”

“Kal mat pehenna phir.”

Shivani ne aankh uthayi. “Main sambhaal lungi.”

Aditya hansa. “Tumhara confidence toh CA office ke bahar bhi full-time kaam karta hai.”

Woh chala gaya. Shivani ne blouse ko alag hanger par rakha.

**READER-ONLY — SHIVANI:** Chotu ka kal aana sirf ek practical errand tha. Shivani ka kal usi blouse ko pehenne ka faisla practical nahi tha. Woh dekhna chahti thi ki kya ek shant, formal Shivani ke alawa koi doosri Shivani bhi uski aankhon mein dikh sakti hai. Dori ki kamzori ya usse hone wali awkwardness ka usne koi plan nahi banaya tha.

Usne khidki band ki aur kitabein sametne lagi. Excitement ke saath ek sankoch bhi tha. Woh kisi baat ka jawaab abhi nahi chahti thi.

### SHREYA — TUESDAY, 9:20 AM · MEHTA BOUTIQUE

Boutique ka naya collection window display mein jaana tha. Shreya ne assistant ko bola tha ki white backdrop aur neutral lighting rakhein. Aaj client fittings kam thi, isliye woh khud first-look photos test karne wali thi.

Uske phone par Aarav ka message aa chuka tha: *Bhabhi, chord practise hui?* Neel ka bhi ek short technical note tha—*Sunday recording level was clean.*

Shreya ne dono messages padhe aur phone ulta rakh diya.

“Ab toh mere music instructors ke reminders bhi aa gaye,” usne khud se kaha. “Fashion wali duniya mein wapas aao, madam.”

Changing room mein usne ek wine-coloured saree nikali. Is collection ka blouse deep V-neck cut ka tha, aur saree ke drape se side-waist ka ek chhota hissa dikhne wala tha. Boutique model ke liye sample bana tha, lekin us par bhi fit aa raha tha.

Woh mirror ke saamne khadi hui aur pallu ki do placements try ki. First placement zyada formal thi. Second mein waistline visible thi aur neckline ka shape saaf dikh raha tha.

Shreya ne apne reflection ko dekhkar ek lambi saans li.

Ravivaar ko Chotu ne ribbon baandhte hue uski singing ki tareef ki thi. Usse pehle fabric roll pakadte waqt woh ek pal ke liye ajeeb tarah se chup hua tha. Woh moment Shreya ko ab tak yaad tha. *Kya woh outfit notice kar raha tha?* Phir usne apne hi sawaal par hansi rok li.

“Main boutique owner hoon. Log mujhe kapdon mein dekhenge hi. Lekin mujhe uska opinion kyun chahiye?”

Assistant ne curtain ke bahar se kaha, “Madam, backdrop wala stand aa gaya. Chirag bhi payment receipt ke saath hai.”

Shreya ne mirror mein seedha khade hokar dekha. “Usse bolo, shoot corner mein rakh de. Main aa rahi hoon.”

Usne pallu ko second placement mein rehne diya. Koi chori-chhipe accident nahi, koi kapde ka malfunction nahi—yeh look usne consciously select kiya tha.

**READER-ONLY — SHREYA:** Shreya ko apne professional design par bharosa tha. Aaj woh ek aise ladke se genuine opinion chahti thi jise usne kabhi fashion critic nahi maana tha. Uske liye curiosity aur attraction ke beech ki rekha ab pehle jaisi aasaan nahi rahi.

Woh bahar nikli. Camera stand abhi khaali tha. Chotu andar aane se pehle delivery sheet dekh raha tha.

Shreya ne khud ko ek second diya. Phir familiar awaaz mein boli, “Chotu Babu, aaj delivery ke saath ek extra assignment bhi hai.”

### TANIYA — TUESDAY, 12:40 PM · ARORA APARTMENT

Taniya ne wedding-game photos ki memory card laptop mein lagayi. Har dusri photo mein kisi na kisi ka face funny tha. Ek photo mein Laksh ka haath hawaa mein tha aur Arjun aankhein band karke speech de raha tha.

“Four intelligent devars,” Taniya ne badbadaaya. “One decent photograph nahi.”

Dev office gaya tha. Ira ne subah call karke poochha tha ki final albums kab milenge. Taniya ne vaada kiya tha ki shaam tak shortlist bhej degi.

Chotu ka message aaya: *Didi, remaining decor sheets lene 2 baje aa jaaun?*

*Aa jao,* Taniya ne reply kiya. *Aur camera phone charge karke lana. Ek kaam bhi hai.*

Woh wardrobe ke saamne aayi. Sunday ki maroon saree laundry ko bhej di thi. Aaj usne rust-red cotton-silk saree chuni, jiska blouse structured fit ka tha. Pallu ko front se pin karne ke bajay, usne side mein softly drape kiya. Mirror mein uski waist ka curve aur saree ke neatly gathered pleats visible the. Side angle se fabric uske hips ka silhouette follow kar raha tha.

Taniya ne khud ko dekhkar muskuraya. “Theek hai, madam. Marketing ka first rule: presentation.”

Usko Chotu ka Sunday wala nervous glance yaad aaya. Phir Monday clubhouse mein Laksh ka joke—*fifth devar competition*. Us waqt usne usse sirf hasi mein uda diya tha. Aaj pata nahi kyun, woh dono moments uske saath chale aaye.

Kya woh Chotu ko tease karna chahti thi? Haan. Kya woh uski reaction ko lekar curious thi? Bilkul. Lekin chaaron devaron se alag, Chotu har joke ke baad ek pal rukta tha—jaise words galat chune toh nuksan ho jayega.

Usne ek photograph kholi jisme woh camera ki taraf dekhkar hans rahi thi. Phir doosri, jo side profile thi.

“Dekhte hain Mister Honest Delivery Boy kya choose karta hai.”

Ira ka phone aaya.

“Bhabhi, Arjun ne kaha aap sari photos reject kar rahi ho.”

“Bilkul. Kuch logon ko camera ke saamne poses seekhne chahiye.”

“Chotu aa raha hai?”

“Haan, sheets ke liye.”

Ira hansi. “Toh Arjun ko bhi bula doon? Competition ho jayega.”

“Nahi,” Taniya ne turant kaha. “Is baar mujhe seedha jawab chahiye, commentary nahi.”

Phone rakhkar woh ek pal ke liye chup hui. Usne Chotu ke saath akele photo opinion lene ka plan khud banaya tha. Is baat ne use thoda surprise kiya.

**READER-ONLY — TANIYA:** Usne outfits aur photographs khud select kiye. Woh apne look ke baare mein Chotu ka honest response chahti thi—aur shayad uski famous teasing ko kisi naye insaan par aazmaana bhi. Lekin reaction ka control uske haath mein nahi tha.

Doorbell abhi nahi baji thi. Taniya ne laptop par do photographs side by side khol di.

*“Aao Chotu Babu. Dekhte hain tum kitne honest ho.”*

### CLOSING INTERCUT — CHIRAG

Society store mein Chirag ne delivery screen kholi. Chaar addresses alag time slots mein the; uske liye ab bhi yeh kaam tha, koi secret itinerary nahi.

Ankita didi ka verification issue aaj 4 baje solve karna tha. Shivani didi ke printed brochures file mein the. Shreya didi ki boutique receipt sambhaalni thi. Taniya didi ne photographs ka kuch extra kaam diya tha.

Use chaaron pasand thi, lekin pasand ka matlab bhi chaaron ke saath alag tha. Ankita ki quick intelligence use impress karti. Shivani ki softness aur discipline, Shreya ki creative confidence, Taniya ki shararat—sab yaad rehta.

Usne screen lock ki.

*“Main sabke saath waise hi baat kar paaunga jaise hamesha karta tha?”*

Sawaal aate hi usne apne aap ko tok diya. Delivery boy ko late nahi hona chahiye.
