# Four Walls, Four Secrets — E01 Part 4 / Shreya (Continuity Revision Draft)

> **Status:** Grok-origin reader-preview draft, mechanically continuity-edited; unapproved, not canon. Source sensory/touch passages retained; see revision log.

Sunday, almost noon.

Shreya ne Courtyard lawn pe step rakha. Plum-coloured boat-neck kurti uske shoulders pe soft 
padi thi, neckline thoda open, silver jhumkas halki si hil rahi thi. Baal half-tied the, thode se loose 
strands face pe aa rahe the.
Aarav pehle dikha. Guitar strap uske shoulder pe tha, uske chehre pe wohi familiar teasing smile.
“Arre, aaj toh poora different look hai,” usne seedha kaha. “Yeh plum colour… aap pe bahut soft 
lag raha hai. Aur yeh jhumke. Kal raat se voice yaad aa rahi thi aapki.”
Shreya ne halki si hasi di. “Main bride-entry cues verify karne aayi hoon, audition dene nahi.”
Neel thoda peeche khada tha, sound board ke paas. Usne quietly kaha, “Timing aapki kal bahut 
clean thi. Aaj final check kar lete hain.”
Uske sleeves rolled up the. Shreya ki nazar ek second ke liye uske toned forearms pe ruk gayi — 
veins thodi si visible, shoulders fitted T-shirt mein sharp. Usne pehle kabhi itna detail se notice 
nahi kiya tha.
Neel uske paas aaya, chhota sa headset mic haath mein.
“Permission…?” usne soft awaaz mein poocha.
Shreya ne haan mein nod kiya.
Woh uske right side se aaya. Uske fingers pehle uske baal ke near gaye, phir mic clip ko carefully 
uske ear ke peeche set karne lage. Uske ungliyan uske outer neck pe halka sa brush ho gayi — 
bilkul earlobe ke neeche, soft skin pe.
Shreya ki count ek beat ke liye ruk si gayi.
Neel itna close tha ki uski warm breath uske neck aur shoulder pe fail rahi thi. Uske fingers ab 
bhi uske ear ke around adjust kar rahe the. Contact bahut light tha, lekin clear. Shreya ke andar 
ek ajeeb si warmth fail gayi. Usne notice kiya ki Neel ke haath kitne shaped the, kitne steady. 
Uske mind mein ek quiet surprise aaya — itne time se jaanti thi usko, lekin aaj pehli baar uska 
physical presence itna register ho raha tha.
“Thoda sa… yahan,” Neel ne dheemi awaaz mein kaha, bilkul uske kaan ke paas.
Shreya ne normal voice mein jawab diya, “Okay… theek hai.” Lekin uske andar ek soft 
bashfulness chal rahi thi. Uske nipples kurti ke neeche thode se aware ho gaye the. Woh 
consciously ignore karne ki koshish kar rahi thi.
Mic set ho gaya. Neel thoda peeche hata, lekin uski nazar ek second ke liye uske face pe ruk i 
thi. Usne notice kiya tha ki uski breathing thodi si change hui thi. Usne kuch nahi kaha.
Phir Aarav aage aaya, guitar leke.
“Ab ek easy chord try karte hain. Last time aapne kaha tha seekhna hai.”

Shreya ne hasi ke saath guitar pakda. Woh kursi pe baith gayi. Aarav uske peeche-side position 
mein aa gaya — uska chest uski back ke bilkul near, lekin touch nahi. Usne uske haath ko 
fretboard pe guide kiya.
Uske fingers uske fingers pe aa gaye. Palm uske forearm se brush hua. Jab usne strum 
demonstrate kiya toh uska upper arm uske shoulder se halka sa touch ho gaya.
“Yahan press karo… thoda firm,” Aarav ne kaha. Uski awaaz uske kaan ke bilkul close thi, playful 
aur encouraging.
Shreya ne galat chord press kar diya. Guitar se odd sound aaya. Woh has padi, cheeks thode se 
pink ho gaye.
Aarav bhi hasa. “Almost… ek baar aur.”
Uske haath phir se uske haath pe aa gaye. Is baar contact thoda zyada der tak raha. Shreya ke 
andar do alag feelings mix ho rahi thi — pehle Neel ka calm, quiet closeness uske neck pe, ab 
Aarav ka cheeky, confident energy uske kaan ke paas aur uske haathon pe.
Woh privately compare kar rahi thi. Dono alag the. Dono uske body mein alag ripple chhod 
rahe the.
Aarav ne last mein soft tone mein kaha, “Quick learner ho aap. Next time kaunsa 
chord seekhenge?”
Shreya ne hasi ke saath deflect kiya. “Pehle yeh wala toh theek se aa jaye.”
Aarav ne guitar stand par rakha. Neel ne final bride-entry cue ek baar aur play kiya; speaker timing aur mic level dono theek the.
“Sound-check clear,” Neel ne confirm kiya. Shreya ne venue sheet par approval tick kar diya. Rehearsal ka actual kaam bhi complete ho chuka tha.
Session wrap hua. Teeno ne ordinary goodbye kiya.
Shreya ghar ki taraf chal padi. Raste mein uske mind mein do moments baar-baar aa rahe the — 
Neel ke fingers uske neck pe, uski warm breath, aur Aarav ka body uske peeche, uske haath 
uske haathon pe, uski awaaz uske kaan mein.
Woh privately soch rahi thi… kya woh sirf music ke liye next time aana chahti thi, ya in dono ko 
dekhne ke liye bhi?
Uttar abhi clear nahi tha. Lekin curiosity definitely aa chuki thi.
