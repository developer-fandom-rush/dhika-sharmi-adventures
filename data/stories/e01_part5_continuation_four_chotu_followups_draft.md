## Ankita — The Unfinished Conversation
*Sunday · 10:15 AM · Bansal Apartment*

Subah Ankita laptop par laundry service ka complaint form bhar rahi thi. Kal shaam ka packet kholne par ek matching innerwear set missing mila tha. Usne dobara check kiya, phir service ko message kiya. Use nahi pata tha ki parcel galat flat mein pahunchne ke baad kya hua tha.

“Mom, rocket ki lights phir band hain!” Rey ki awaaz sunkar woh hans padi.

“Tumhara rocket Mars tak pahunchne se pehle retirement le lega.”

Usi samay doorbell baji. Chotu batteries ka replacement lekar khada tha.

“Good morning, didi!”

“Good morning, Chotu Babu. Kal ke rocket engineer ko phir tumhari zaroorat hai.”

Ankita ne use andar bulaya. Aaj usne soft cotton kurti pehni thi. Baal dheele bandhe the aur chehre par pichhli raat ki thakaan thi.

Chotu ne samaan rakhkar Rey ke saath circuit check karna shuru kiya.

Ankita ne use kaam karte dekha. Kal woh achanak itna chup kyun ho gaya tha? Sawaal abhi bhi uske mann mein tha.

“Chotu, kal kuch hua tha kya? Ekdum silent ho gaye the.”

“Arre nahi didi. Bas aap logon ka rocket dekh raha tha.”

“Achha? Itna complicated hai hamara rocket?”

“Engineer sahab ka project hai. Darna toh padega!”

Ankita hans di.

Kuch der baad usne dekha ki Chotu apne phone par kisi online application ko samajhne ki koshish kar raha hai.

“Dikhao.”

“Didi, store ke liye ek delivery app ka form hai. Samajh nahi aa raha.”

Ankita uske paas baithkar form samjhane lagi.

“Ye dekho, har field ke neeche example diya hai. Pehle address bharo, phir documents upload karo.”

Chotu chupchaap uski baat sunta raha.

Kal usne Ankita ki appearance ko alag nazar se notice kiya tha. Aaj uski confidence aur madad karne ke andaaz ne use aur prabhavit kiya.

“Didi, aap sab kitni aasani se samjha deti ho.”

“Professional habit hai, Chotu Babu.”

Rey beech mein bola, “Mumma best teacher hain!”

Ankita muskuraayi.

Chotu ne jaate-jaate dhanyavaad kaha. Woh phir normal lag raha tha. Lekin Ankita ko kal shaam ki uski achanak badli expression yaad thi.

Aur laundry complaint ka jawaab abhi tak nahi aaya tha.

## Shivani — The Breakfast Invitation
*Sunday · 9:30 AM · Sinha Apartment*

Shivani ne bathroom ka darwaaza band karke ek baar phir drying stand ki taraf dekha. Kal wahan rakhi uski bra ki position kuch alag lagi thi.

“Shayad maine hi jaldi mein theek se nahi rakhi,” usne socha. Phir baat taal di.

Kitchen mein Aditya ke liye breakfast taiyaar tha. Use wedding preparations mein jaldi nikalna tha.

“Shivani, main hall jaa raha hoon. Tum aaram se aana,” Aditya ne kaha.

“Okay. Aur please accounts waali file car mein mat bhoolna.”

Uske jaane ke kuch der baad doorbell baji. Chotu dukaan se aaya tha. Haath mein kal ka pending delivery invoice aur ek chhota notebook tha.

“Didi, ek help chahiye thi.”

“Bolo, Chotu.”

“Store ke hisaab mein ek amount match nahi ho raha.”

Shivani ne notebook liya.

“Tumhe calculator chalana aata hai?”

“Didi!”

“Achha baba, mazaak kar rahi hoon.”

Dono hans diye.

Shivani ne use dining table par bithaya aur do entries ka farq samjhaya. Chotu dhyaan se sunta raha.

Woh uthne laga toh Shivani ne poochha, “Breakfast kiya?”

“Ghar jaakar kar loonga.”

“Baitho. Poha bana hai. Main bhi kha rahi hoon.”

Chotu ne pehle sankoch kiya.

“Didi, aap logon ko disturb...”

“Arre, accountant hoon. Ek plate extra ka hisaab manage kar lungi.”

Is baar woh hanskar baith gaya.

Khaana khaate hue usne bataya ki dukaan ki deliveries aur ghar ki responsibilities ke beech padhaai phir shuru karna chahta hai.

Shivani kuch der chup rahi.

“Tum seriously padhna chahte ho toh main course options dekhne mein help kar sakti hoon.”

Chotu ne uski taraf dekha. “Sach, didi?”

“Haan. Lekin mehnat tumhari hogi.”

Kal usne Shivani ko achanak asahaj sthiti mein dekha tha. Aaj use mehsoos hua ki uski shaant aur sankochi appearance ke peeche kitna aatmavishwas hai.

Shivani ke liye yeh ek saamanya madad thi. Chotu ke liye shayad thodi adhik yaadgaar.

## Shreya — The Boutique Compliment
*Sunday · 6:30 PM · Shreya's Boutique*

Shaam tak Shreya thak chuki thi. Subah Neel ke saath sound-check aur Aarav se guitar seekhne waala chhota lesson ab bhi uske dimaag mein ghoom raha tha.

Boutique mein woh fabric rolls arrange kar rahi thi. Dopahar ko Chotu ke saamne oonchi shelf se roll nikaalte samay kurti thoda upar khisakne waala awkward moment bhi yaad aaya.

“Madam, aaj kaafi busy lag rahi hain.”

Darwaaze se Chotu ki awaaz aayi. Woh packaging ka bacha samaan lene aaya tha.

“Busy nahi, Chotu. Exhausted.”

“Phir bhi aap sab manage kar leti ho.”

“Bas, ab tum bhi compliments shuru mat karna!”

Chotu hans pada.

Shreya ne ek gift hamper nikaala.

“Achha, tumhari madad chahiye. Ye ribbon pakdo.”

Chotu ne ribbon pakda. Shreya ne use decorative knot banaana sikhaya.

“Dekho, is corner ko fold karna hai. Zyada tight nahi.”

“Didi, mere se toh grocery bag hi theek pack hote hain.”

“Practice karo. Boutique assistant bana dungi.”

Dono hanse.

Chotu ki nazar Shreya ke haathon ki tez aur saaf movements par gayi. Use yaad aaya ki yeh wahi Shreya thi jo wedding hall mein har volunteer ko bina ghabraaye nirdesh deti thi.

“Waise didi, aaj aapne gaana bhi gaya tha?”

Shreya ruk gayi. “Tumne suna?”

“Thoda sa. Bahut achcha tha.”

Is baar Shreya sachmuch muskura di.

Neel aur Aarav ne bhi uski awaaz ki tareef ki thi. Lekin Chotu se aisi baat sunne ki use ummeed nahi thi.

“Thank you, Chotu.”

“Phir kab gaaogi?”

“Pehle guitar seekh loon!”

“Guitar bhi?”

Shreya hanste hue boli, “Haan. Mere talents ki list abhi complete nahi hui.”

Chotu ke jaane ke baad usne ribbon waala hamper dekha.

Use achcha laga ki log uske kaam ke alaawa uske chhipe hue shauk bhi notice kar rahe the.

## Taniya — Five Against One
*Monday · 11 AM · Clubhouse Cleanup*

Wedding event khatam ho chuka tha, lekin clubhouse mein abhi bhi samaan ke boxes rakhe the.

Taniya ne clipboard uthaya.

“Vihaan, Arjun, Dhruv, Laksh! Tum chaaron kal wedding games ke heroes the. Aaj cleanup se bhaag kyun rahe ho?”

Laksh ne naatkiya andaaz mein haath jod diye.

“Bhabhi, heroes ko recovery day milna chahiye.”

“Achha? Toh recovery ke paise bhi kaat loon?”

Sab hans pade.

Tabhi Chotu khaali cartons lene aaya.

“Good morning, Taniya didi.”

“Arey Chotu! Tum aa gaye. At least ek responsible ladka toh hai yahan.”

Arjun ne turant kaha, “Bhabhi, hum chaaron ki insult ho rahi hai!”

Taniya hansi. “Insult nahi, performance evaluation.”

Chotu ne bade cartons uthaane shuru kiye. Taniya ek table ke paas khadi hokar use labels bata rahi thi.

Kal ki storeroom ghatna ke baad woh saavdhaani se chal rahi thi. Laksh ne bhi yaad dilaya:

“Bhabhi, floor par koi kapda ho toh pehle mujhe bula lena.”

“Bilkul nahi! Tumhari rescue service ki rating bahut kharaab hai.”

Sab phir hans pade.

Chotu kuch der chupchaap kaam karta raha.

Taniya ne poochha, “Kya hua? Tum bhi inke jokes se darr gaye?”

“Nahi didi. Bas aap kal se itna kaam kar rahi ho. Thakti nahi?”

Taniya ek pal ko ruki.

“Thakti hoon, Chotu. Bas in chaaron ke saamne admit nahi karti.”

Vihaan ne uski taraf dekha. “Bhabhi, hum help toh kar rahe hain.”

“Kar rahe ho. Lekin supervision ke bina tum chaaron ka result doubtful hai.”

Arjun ne mazaak mein kaha, “Chotu ko fifth devar bana lo. Team strong ho jayegi.”

Laksh turant bola, “Nahi bhai, phir favourite bhabhi ka favourite devar competition badh jayega.”

Taniya ne clipboard band kar diya.

“Bas! Ab jo sabse pehle boxes finish karega, usko chai milegi.”

“Bhabhi, sabko!” Laksh chillaaya.

“Dekhenge!”

Kaam phir shuru ho gaya.

Taniya ko unke saath ki familiar teasing achchi lag rahi thi. Lekin kal ke kuch achanak kareeb aaye kshan bhi use yaad the—khaaskar jab sabhi use girne se bachaane daude the.

Woh nahi jaanti thi ki kis ladke ke mann mein kaun-si chhoti baat reh gayi hai.

Aur Chotu ab unke beech ek naya parichit chehra ban chuka tha.
