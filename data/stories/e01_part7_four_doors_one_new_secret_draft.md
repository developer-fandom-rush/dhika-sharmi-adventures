# Four Walls, Four Secrets
## Episode 01 · Part 7 — Chaar Darwaaze, Ek Naya Raaz
*Full reader-preview prose · Tuesday · continuation of Part 6: Chaar Faisle · not reader-locked final canon*

### 1. SHREYA — THE SECOND PHOTOGRAPH
*Tuesday · 9:25 AM · Mehta Boutique*

Boutique ki front window par subah ki dhoop ek patli si dhaar banakar gir rahi thi. Assistant ne safed backdrop ko do clamps se seedha kiya aur doosri taraf se steamer ki halki seeti sunai di. Shreya changing-room se nikli toh ek pal ke liye apni hi shop use nayi lagi.

Wine-coloured saree ka pallu usne carefully shoulder par rakha tha. Fitted blouse ka V-shaped neckline aur saree ke side drape ke beech thodi bare waist nazar aati thi. Ye wahi second styling thi jo usne kuch der pehle mirror mein choose ki thi. Woh is collection ko display karne wali designer thi; phir bhi aaj use lag raha tha jaise design ke saath-saath uska apna confidence bhi camera ke saamne test hone wala hai.

“Chotu Babu,” usne doorway se pukara, “aaj tumhe delivery ke paise ke alawa ek aur kaam karna padega.”

Chirag ne receipt fold kar di. “Didi, pehle bata do, iske bhi overtime milega?”

“Ek chocolate aur ek honest opinion.”

“Honest opinion free hota hai?”

“Bilkul nahi. Galat diya toh phir se photo leni padegi.”

Assistant hans padi aur customer-call lene reception par chali gayi. Chotu background stand ke paas aa gaya. Shreya ne uske haath mein boutique ka phone diya, camera khola aur floor par ek chalk mark dikhaya.

“Yahan se lena. Bas dhyaan rahe saree ka colour natural aaye.”

Chotu ne screen par frame set kiya. “Aap thoda left... nahi, apna left.”

“Photography seekh rahe ho ya traffic control?”

“Dono difficult hain, didi.”

Shreya hansi. Usne pehle formal pose diya: kandhe seedhe, pallu neatly arranged, haath mein ek folded clutch. Click ki awaaz hui.

“Ab second styling,” usne kaha.

Woh mirror ki taraf ghoomi, pallu ko halka sa badla aur wapas camera ki taraf aayi. Is angle mein blouse ka neckline aur side-waist ka contour thoda zyada clear tha. Chotu ne kuch kehne ke liye munh khola, phir phone ki screen dekhi. Shreya ne us chhoti si hesitation ko pakad liya.

“Camera theek hai?”

“Ji. Bas light idhar better hai.”

“Sure?”

“Haan... aur ye look pehle se zyada achcha lag raha hai.”

Shreya ne uski taraf seedha dekha. “Design achcha hai, ya photograph?”

Chotu ki muskaan aadhi reh gayi. “Dono. Aapne jo style kiya hai, woh bhi.”

“Good answer,” Shreya ne kaha. “Lekin main sach mein poochh rahi hoon.”

Chotu ne phone use dikhaya. “Pehle photo mein aap bilkul store ki owner lag rahi ho. Doosre mein collection zyada yaad rehta hai. Shayad isi liye.”

Shreya ne dono tasveerein saath dekhin. Usse laga tha woh bas koi sharmaya hua compliment sunegi; Chotu ne unexpectedly kaam ki baat kar di thi.

“Tumne toh genuine critique de diya.”

“Didi, aapne honest bola tha.”

Shreya ne uske haath se phone liya. “Aur agar main poochhun ki main kaisi lag rahi hoon?”

Is baar Chotu ne jaldi jawaab nahi diya. Store mein steamer band hui toh kuch seconds ki khamoshi aur saaf sunai di.

“Bahut sundar,” usne kaha, phir nazar phone par kar li. “Mera matlab—outfit bhi.”

Shreya ke gaal par halka rang aa gaya. Usne hansi mein baat sambhali. “Theek hai. Photographer ko promotion mil sakta hai.”

Magar phone rakhte hue woh khud hairan thi. Usne yeh sawaal professional feedback ke liye nahi poochha tha. Usse apna hi seedha sawaal yaad reh gaya: *Main kaisi lag rahi hoon?*

Chotu ko pehli baar laga ki Shreya sirf clients ke liye images nahi bana rahi. Uski awaaz mein ek personal curiosity bhi thi. Lekin usne us curiosity ka meaning assume nahi kiya. Woh usse ek saal se jaanta tha—Mahi ke liye chocolate, boutique ke urgent tape rolls, music rehearsal ki baatein. Aaj ki tareef kisi aur promise ke barabar nahi thi.

“Didi, ye photos main aapko hi bhej doon? Gallery mein save nahi karunga,” usne poochha.

Shreya ka expression soft ho gaya. “Haan. Ye important hai. Thank you.”

Usne phone par photographs check ki aur aakhir doosri image select kar li.

Chotu jaane laga toh Shreya ne awaaz di. “Waise, ribbon waala knot seekh gaye?”

“Abhi bhi practice chahiye.”

“Toh kabhi next delivery pe sikha dungi.”

Chotu ne haan mein sir hilaaya. Door band hua toh Shreya ne apni selected photograph phir dekhi. Background saaf tha, colour perfect. Lekin uske liye sabse unusual baat tasveer nahi thi—woh sawaal tha jo usne bina rehearsing poochh liya tha.

**MAJOR EVENT — SHREYA:** She consciously chooses the bolder of two boutique looks, asks Chotu for a personal opinion, receives a sincere compliment, and sets a clear boundary about keeping photographs on her own device. The second image becomes the collection's selected shot.

### 2. TANYA — THE HONEST JUDGE
*Tuesday · 2:00 PM · Arora Apartment*

Tanya ne dining table par teen cheezein rakhi thin: remaining decorative sheets ka bundle, wedding album ka laptop aur paani ka glass. Aaj usne kisi devar ko nahi bulaya tha. Ek ghante ke liye na Vihaan ka over-serious concern chahiye tha, na Arjun ki speech, na Dhruv ki careful silence, na Laksh ka running commentary.

Doorbell baji.

“Entry permitted,” Tanya ne darwaaza kholte hue kaha, “lekin judge ko sach bolna padega.”

Chotu ne bundle uthakar dikhaya. “Judge? Main toh delivery lene aaya tha.”

“Promotion mil gaya.”

Rust-red cotton-silk saree mein Tanya usual wedding-prep maroon se alag lag rahi thi. Blouse neatly fitted tha; pallu side mein drape tha. Walk karte waqt pleats ek saaf silhouette banati thin. Chotu ne ek pal us look ko notice kiya, phir respectfully bundle table par rakha.

Tanya ne uski aankhon ki chhoti si movement pakad li, par turant uspar comment nahi kiya.

“Sit. Do photos compare karni hain.”

Laptop par pehli image khuli: Tanya front-facing, game cards haath mein, laughter ke beech. Doosri image mein woh side se standing microphone adjust kar rahi thi; saree ki waistline, drape aur posture zyada prominent the.

“Now choose. No diplomacy.”

Chotu ne pehli dekhi. “Ismein smile natural hai.”

“Second?”

“Ismein aap confident lag rahi ho. Jaise aap hi poora event chala rahi thi.”

“Which, by the way, is true,” Tanya ne seedha jawaab diya.

“Dono sahi hain.”

“Wrong answer. One photo.”

Chotu sochne laga. Tanya ne chin par haath rakha aur bilkul interviewer ki tarah uske saamne baith gayi.

“Mere chaar devar do minute mein meri tareef karke bhaag jaate. Tum itna analysis kyun karte ho?”

“Kyuki aap phir poochhogi—*kyun?*”

Tanya zor se hansi. “Smart.”

Chotu ne cursor doosri image par rakha. “Ye. Ismein aap photo ke liye khadi nahi ho. Aap kuch kar rahi ho. Achcha lagta hai.”

Tanya ka haath trackpad par ruk gaya.

Use teasing ki ummeed thi. Shayad kisi nervous compliment ki bhi. Lekin Chotu ne us cheez ko choose kiya tha jisse woh khud sabse zyada identify karti thi—kaam karte waqt ka confidence.

“Tumne notice kiya?” usne halka sa poochha.

“Didi, aap jab kaam karti ho na, baaki sabko kaam karna padta hai.”

“Is that a compliment or a complaint?”

“Dono ho sakta hai.”

Tanya ne use fake glare diya. Phir woh uthi aur shelf se ek print envelope nikaalne lagi. Saree ka pallu thoda adjust karte hue usne side profile mein wall mirror dekha. Use apni appearance ko lekar aaj ki consciously ki gayi preparation yaad thi. Chotu ki nazar phir uski taraf aayi, lekin ab woh sirf uski taraf dekhne ke liye nahi, balki envelope pakadne ke liye khada tha.

“Tanya didi, main help kar doon?”

“Arre, mujhe sab aata hai.”

“Pata hai.”

Woh ek saath hans pade.

“Chotu,” Tanya ne envelope dete hue kaha, “tum in chaaron se alag ho.”

“Achcha alag ya bura alag?”

“Abhi judge ka result confidential hai.”

Chotu ke dil mein ek chhoti si khushi hui. Use Tanya ki personality attractive lagti thi—woh bina soch-vichaar ke nahi, khulkar baat karti thi. Lekin uski jokes ko invitation samajh lena galat hoga, yeh bhi woh jaanta tha.

Door ke paas pahunchte hi bell baji. Laksh tha.

“Bhabhi! Photos final hui ki nahi?” Usne Chotu ko dekha. “Oh ho, special jury!”

Tanya ne bina expression badle kaha, “Haan. Aur is jury ne teen minute mein better decision liya. Tum log teen din se argue kar rahe the.”

Laksh ne dramatic sigh li. “Fifth competitor officially appointed.”

Chotu muskuraya, magar Laksh ki aankhon mein curiosity thi. Tanya ne dono ko kaam de diya—“Tum envelope neeche drop karo, tum game cards ka box le jao.” Joke ke peeche ki odd awkwardness turant routine mein badal gayi.

Chotu lift mein khada tha toh use Tanya ka sawaal yaad aaya: *Tumne notice kiya?* Usne khud se kaha ki haan, usne notice kiya tha—sirf saree nahi, uski attention ko apni taraf kheench lene wali confidence bhi.

**MAJOR EVENT — TANYA:** A planned one-on-one photo review becomes a surprisingly honest personal exchange. Chotu chooses Tanya's candid working photograph rather than simply flattering her appearance. Laksh notices the private jury and gains a reason to tease them later.

### 3. ANKITA — THE UNFINISHED SENTENCE
*Tuesday · 4:03 PM · Bansal Apartment*

Ankita ne laptop par delivery-app ka verification page khola aur Chotu ka message padha: *Gate par hoon, didi.*

“Come up,” usne reply kiya.

Ghar mein unusually shanti thi. Rey school ke baad science club mein tha; Ritvik ka office call chal raha tha, par woh ghar se bahar tha. Coffee table par kal ke rocket ka cardboard body aur screwdriver rakha tha. Ankita ne ek baar use dekha aur muskura di. Pichhli baar bhi isi project ne Chotu ko apni baat beech mein rokne par majboor kiya tha.

Doorbell.

“Good afternoon, engineer sahab,” Ankita ne kaha.

“Didi, engineer toh Rey hai. Main assistant hoon.”

“Assistant ke verification documents complete?”

“Almost.”

Woh phone lekar sofa ke ek end par baith gaya. Ankita opposite side se laptop screen uski taraf ghumane lagi. Aaj usne soft blue sleeveless cotton kurti pehni thi—wahi jo kal mirror ke saamne choose ki thi. Loose knot se baalon ki kuch lattain neck ke paas chhoot aayi thin.

“OTP tumhare number par aayega. Kisi ko forward mat karna,” Ankita samjha rahi thi. “Main bhi code nahi dekhungi. Tum khud type karna.”

Chotu ne hairan hokar kaha, “Aap toh mujhe sab help kar deti ho.”

“Help aur password share karna alag cheezein hain. Remember that.”

Usne sir hilaaya.

Verification page load hone laga. Ankita ne charger socket check kiya toh ek cable coffee table ke neeche atki nazar aayi. Uske paas Rey ke rocket ka ek plastic fin bhi pada tha.

“Wait, ye bhi nikaal doon. Nahi toh Rey phir poora ghar search karega.”

Woh table ke paas jhuki. Kurti ki neckline us posture mein thodi shift hui; Chotu ki nazar ek lamhe ko uthi aur turant phone ki screen par laut gayi. Ankita ko uski khamoshi sunaai di—waise hi jaise Saturday ko hui thi.

Fin uske haath mein tha, lekin woh ek second ruk gayi.

*“Haan. Maine sahi notice kiya tha.”*

“Chotu,” usne seedhi hote hue kaha, “tum mujhse koi baat kehna chahte ho?”

“Ji? Nahi didi. OTP aa gaya.”

“Main form ki baat nahi kar rahi.”

Chotu ne uski taraf dekha. Ankita ke tone mein daant nahi thi, lekin uske liye jawaab dena aasaan bhi nahi tha.

“Bas... kabhi kabhi aap bahut different lagti ho,” usne kaha.

“Different? Monday ke audit ki tarah?”

“Arre nahi. Matlab jab aap office ki baat karti ho toh bilkul serious. Rey ke saath toh...” Woh ruk gaya.

“...toh?”

“Bahut happy lagti ho.”

Ankita hansi, lekin phir chhoti si khamoshi hui.

“Kya tum har baar apni baat aadhi chhod dete ho, Chotu Babu?”

“Sirf aapke saamne,” uske munh se nikal gaya.

Dono ek pal chup ho gaye.

Ankita ko us jawaab ki ummeed nahi thi. Usne table par fin rakha aur laptop ki taraf mud gayi.

“Good,” usne halka sa kaha. “Ab baaki aadhi baat bolna bhi seekho.”

Chotu ne saans chhodi. “Didi, aaj aap bahut achchi lag rahi ho.”

Ankita ne seedha uski taraf dekha. “Thank you.”

Is baar woh muskaan kisi accidental gaze se nahi aayi thi. Woh ek compliment ka simple, conscious response tha. Ankita ne nazar nahi churaayi. Phir professional tone mein boli, “Now finish your OTP.”

Chotu ne code enter kiya. Screen par *Verification Successful* aa gaya. Usne khushi se phone uthaya.

“Ho gaya!”

“Congratulations,” Ankita ne kaha. “Ab app ka customer support meri tarah har baar free nahi milega.”

“Nahi didi, main aapko disturb nahi karunga.”

“Disturb karna aur genuine help poochhna alag hai. Samjhe?”

Usne sir hilaaya.

Chotu jab jaane laga toh Ankita ne usse Rey ka rocket fin bag mein rakhne se roka. “Ye rehne do. Tumhara kaam complete. Rey ka kaam usko karne do.”

Door band hone ke baad woh kuch seconds wahi khadi rahi. Chotu ne use achchi kaha tha. Is baat se zyada use apne khud ke jawaab ne surprise kiya tha: ek seedha, bina chhupe *thank you*.

Laptop par laundry service ka ek naya email aaya tha: *We are unable to account for the missing items and are escalating internally.* Ankita ne ise star mark kar diya. Woh raaz abhi alag tha; Chotu ke saath ki baat se uska koi sambandh nahi.

Chotu lift mein apne phone par successful verification ki screen dekh raha tha. Khushi thi, lekin usse Ankita ki woh line zyada yaad thi: *Ab baaki aadhi baat bolna bhi seekho.*

Use uski taraf khinchav mehsoos hua. Saath hi usne samjha ki friendly teasing ka matlab koi hidden permission nahi hota. Jo kehna hoga, kabhi bhi seedha aur respectfully kehna hoga—warna kuch baatein dil mein hi rehni chahiye.

**MAJOR EVENT — ANKITA:** Her first consciously chosen appearance experiment leads to a stopped sentence, direct compliment, and an unguarded thank-you. The professional form is genuinely completed; the Part 3 laundry mystery remains separate and unresolved.

### 4. SHIVANI — THE BROKEN DORI
*Tuesday · 6:58 PM · Sinha Apartment*

Shaam ke saath hi Sinha apartment mein kitchen se tadke ki halki khushboo aa rahi thi. Shivani ne dining table par brochures aur course comparison ki ek sheet rakhi. Chotu saat baje aane wala tha.

Usne cream saree pehni thi. Matching blouse ka back kaafi open tha, do narrow doriyon ke saath. Baal upar bun mein the; uske kandhon aur upper back ka design mirror mein saaf nazar aa raha tha. Front fasteners alag se secure the. Monday ko wardrobe ke saamne khade hokar jo faisla kiya tha, woh aaj outfit ban chuka tha.

Shivani ne back mirror mein dori ko dekha. Knot uske haath se ठीक से नहीं बन रहा था। Pichhle hafte usi dori ka ek thread nikal aaya tha. Aditya ki warning yaad aayi—*Tailor ko dena.* Usne ek baar socha, kapde badal loon. Phir khud se poochha, *Main itni nervous kyun hoon?*

Doorbell ne sawaal adhura chhod diya.

“Good evening, didi,” Chotu ne darwaaze par kaha. “Brochures aur Aditya bhaiya ki file.”

“Time pe aaye. Impressive.”

“Main professional hoon.”

“Achha? Toh course list bhi professional banaayi hai?”

Woh dining table ke paas aaya. Shivani ne papers kholkar fees aur admission dates dikhayi. Dono ka dhyaan kuch minutes tak entirely documents par raha. Chotu genuinely interested tha. Use ek certification pathway samajh aaya jo kaam ke saath kiya ja sakta tha.

“Didi, shayad ye one-year option sahi rahega.”

“Reason?”

“Job chhodna nahi padega, aur fees installments mein hai.”

“Excellent. Ab tum khud decide karo. Main sirf explain kar sakti hoon.”

Chotu ne muskura kar sir hilaaya. Usne Shivani ka new outfit notice kiya tha, lekin uske liye aaj documents ki urgency bhi real thi. Shivani uski careful nazar aur formal behaviour dono notice kar rahi thi.

Papers samet kar woh uthi toh dori phir loose mehsoos hui. Shivani ne ek pal ki hesitation ke baad kaha, “Chotu, ek practical help karoge?”

“Ji?”

“Blouse ke peeche ki dori ka knot loose hai. Main reach nahi kar paa rahi. Agar tum comfortable ho toh ise halka sa baandh doge? Nahi toh rehne do.”

Chotu ek second ke liye startled hua. “Main? Didi, aap sure ho?”

“Haan. Bas dori hai. Aur dheere se.”

Woh uske peeche ek respectful distance par khada hua. Shivani ne pallu aage sambhaala aur seedhi khadi rahi. Chotu ne sirf do strings pakdi. Dori uski ungliyon mein surprisingly thin thi. Knot banate waqt uski fingertips ek pal Shivani ki upper back ko chhoo gayin.

Shivani ne aaine mein uski focused expression dekhi. Woh kuch kehne wali thi ki ek halki si *tchik* ki awaaz hui.

Dori ka weak thread toot gaya.

“Didi!” Chotu ne turant haath hata liya. “Sorry, sorry! Maine zor nahi lagaya. Sach mein!”

Shivani chonk kar palti. Blouse ke front aur main back fasteners secure the; kapda apni jagah tha. Sirf decorative dori ka loose hissa Chotu ke haath mein reh gaya tha.

“Arey, rukho. Theek hai,” usne kaha. “Ye pehle se kharaab thi.”

Chotu ka chehra bilkul utar gaya. “Mujhe help nahi karni chahiye thi. Sorry.”

“Chotu, dekho meri taraf. Tumne kuch galat nahi kiya. Main hi tailor ke paas le jaana bhool gayi.”

Lekin woh pehle hi do steps peeche hat chuka tha.

“Main... main chalta hoon didi. Brochure ka message kar dena.”

“Ek minute—”

“Sorry.”

Darwaaza band ho gaya.

Shivani ne tooti dori ko dekha, phir apne reflection ko. Woh Chotu ki reaction dekhna chahti thi. Usne socha bhi nahi tha ki ek chhoti si tailoring problem dono ko itna awkward kar degi.

Usne pallu pin kiya aur phone uthaya. Message likha: *Chotu, don't worry. Dori already weak thi. Thanks for bringing the documents. Ghar safely pahunchkar message kar dena.*

Send.

Chotu lift mein phone ki notification dekhkar ruka. Usne read kiya, lekin reply tab diya jab neeche pahunch gaya: *Pahunch gaya didi. Sorry again. Course list ke liye thank you.*

Shivani ko hansi bhi aayi aur thodi kasak bhi. Usne message ka jawaab diya: *Ab sorry quota khatam. Good night.*

Us raat usne blouse alag rakh diya—tailor ke liye, kisi elaborate excuse ke liye nahi.

Woh jaanti thi ki usne Chotu ke aane se pehle outfit consciously select kiya tha. Woh yeh bhi jaanti thi ki dori tootna koi planned scene nahi tha. Dono baaton ko ek hi category mein rakhkar khud ko confuse nahi karna chahti thi.

Chotu ke liye bhi woh moment alag tha. Shivani ki trust ne use surprise kiya tha; accident ne usse embarrassment di. Uske dil mein attraction thi, lekin is shaam ki sabse tez memory uske chehre ki smile nahi—uska *“tumne kuch galat nahi kiya”* tha.

**MAJOR EVENT — SHIVANI:** She chooses a distinct open-back outfit, asks Chotu for a specific, limited help, a previously weakened decorative string snaps, and he leaves apologizing. The outfit remains secure. Their later messages clarify that it was an accident; trust survives the embarrassment.

### 5. CHOTU — FOUR RECEIPTS
*Tuesday · 9:22 PM · Society convenience store*

Dukaan bandh hone ko thi. Chirag ne refrigerator ki light off ki, cash drawer balance kiya aur notebook ke last page par chaar pending reminders likhe:

*Mehta boutique — photographs sent. Arora apartment — decor sheets collected. Bansal flat — app verified. Sinha flat — course option follow-up.*

Kaam ke hisaab se ordinary Tuesday tha. Ek bhi payment pending nahi thi.

Magar uske dimaag mein din ordinary nahi tha.

Shreya didi ne phone par apni do photographs compare karte waqt use ek aisa sawaal poochha tha jiska jawaab usne school ki kisi exam mein bhi itna sochkar nahi diya hota. Tanya didi ne uske decision ko jaanchne ke liye fake courtroom bana diya tha. Ankita didi ne uski aadhi baat complete karne ko kaha tha. Shivani didi ki dori tootne ke baad usse laga tha ki woh phir kabhi unke saamne normal nahi ho payega—phir unka message aaya aur saans aasaan hui.

Usne khidki ke sheeshe mein apna reflection dekha.

“Chirag,” usne dheere se kaha, “tumhe kya ho raha hai?”

Woh samajh raha tha ki chaaron women ek doosre se kitni alag hain. Woh unhe ek single fantasy ya ek identical reaction mein nahi jod sakta tha. Har ek ki family, apna kaam, apni history thi. Aur unki attention ka har pal alag wajah se aa sakta tha—kindness, professional trust, mazaak, curiosity.

Phir bhi attraction ko jhooth bolkar mitaaya nahi ja sakta tha. Use kuch aise pal yaad the jab uska dil tez dhadka tha, jab jawaab dene mein der lag gayi thi, jab kisi ki hansi uske saath lift tak aa gayi thi. Woh yeh bhi jaanta tha ki uske private thoughts kisi doosre ki taraf se ki gayi declaration nahi hain.

Usne notebook band ki.

Phone par Rey ke science project ka ek photo aaya: rocket finally lit up. Message Ankita ka tha—*Engineer Rey says thanks to assistant Chotu.*

Chirag hans pada. Turant ek aur notification: Shivani ne course ka prospectus forward kar diya tha.

Usne dono ko short, normal replies diye. Baaki do conversations uske mind mein bina message ke hi zinda thin.

Dukaan ki shutter neeche karte hue woh ruka. Ek pal ko use laga uski life chaar guna complicated ho gayi hai. Phir usne khud ko correct kiya.

*“Nahi. Chaar alag kahaaniyan meri roz ki zindagi ka hissa ban gayi hain. Mujhe dhyaan se chalna hoga.”*

### 6. THE FOUR WALLS — EPISODE 01 CODA
*Tuesday · late night · four homes*

Bansal apartment mein Ankita ne laptop band kiya aur laundry complaint ke folder ko archive nahi kiya. Usne missing set ka issue solve hone tak khula chhodne ka faisla kiya. Uske zehan mein Chotu ki aadhi baat aur ek seedhi tareef thi, lekin woh kisi ko ek doosre ka substitute nahi samajh rahi thi.

Sinha apartment mein Shivani ne damaged blouse tailor-bag mein rakha, phir study table par course brochures ki printout seedhi ki. Sheesha uske saamne tha, lekin aaj usse apne reflection se zyada apne decision ka khayal aa raha tha.

Mehta home mein Shreya ne boutique ke liye second photograph select karke assistant ko bheji. Mahi so chuki thi. Aarav ka next guitar lesson message unread pada tha; Neel ka technical note already filed. Chotu ka sincerely diya opinion yaad aate hi woh phir muskuraayi.

Arora apartment mein Tanya ne Laksh ke teen voice notes mute kiye. Wedding album ke cover ke liye Chotu ki chosen photograph ab shortlist mein thi. Dev ne poochha, “Final photo mil gayi?” Tanya ne kaha, “Haan. Finally ek honest judge mil gaya.”

Charon gharon mein ek aadmi ka naam alag meanings ke saath yaad aaya—Chotu. Lekin har woman ke paas aaj ki shaam ke baare mein apna jawaab tha; kisi ne doosri ko apna secret nahi bataya.

Aur ek doosra layer bhi tha, jiske baare mein unmein se koi poori tarah nahi jaanti thi: Part 3 ke older men ke alag, hidden misconduct. Woh pehle hua tha. Uska Chotu ke visits se koi documented connection nahi tha. Missing parcel aur displaced garments ka sach abhi saamne aana baaki tha.

Door band ho gaye. Raaz band nahi hue.
