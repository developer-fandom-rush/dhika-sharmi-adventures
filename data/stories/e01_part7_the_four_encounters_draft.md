## Episode 01 — Part 7: Chaar Mulaqatein

### ANKITA — TUESDAY, 4:02 PM · BANSAL APARTMENT

Doorbell bajne se pehle Ankita ne teen baar phone dekha. Pehli baar client message ke liye. Doosri baar verification code ke liye. Teesri baar bina kisi khaas wajah ke.

Woh khud apni harkat par muskurayi.

Rey ki rocket body ab coffee table ke neeche nahi thi. Usne subah hi use shelf par rakh diya tha, lekin tape aur batteries ka dabba abhi living room mein tha. Soft-blue cotton kurti mein Ankita comparatively relaxed lag rahi thi. Sleeveless cut aur simple neckline office ke structured kapdon se bilkul alag the. Uska stole side chair par tha.

“Good afternoon, didi!” Chotu ne darwaaze se kaha. “Form ne phir attack kar diya.”

“Form tumpar attack nahi kar raha, Chirag. Tum uski instructions skip kar rahe ho.”

“Chirag mat bolo, didi. Lagta hai principal office mein bula rahe hain.”

Ankita hans padi. “Theek hai, Chotu Babu. Phone dikhao.”

Dono dining table ke paas baith gaye. Chotu ne screen kholi: *Business verification pending.* Ankita ne instructions padhkar poochha, “Store ka registered number kaunsa hai?”

“Maami ka. Unhone OTP bheja tha. Main usse submit kar chuka hoon.”

“Good. Ab yahan additional proof chahiye. Woh receipt?”

Chotu ne apna bag khola, lekin receipt neeche gir gayi. Ankita uske saath jhukkar kagaz uthane lagi. Ek pal ko dono ke haath ek hi sheet ke kinare par aa gaye. Chotu ne turant haath peeche kar liya.

“Didi, aap rehne do. Main uthata hoon.”

Ankita ne kagaz seedha karke use diya. “Itni formalities? Kal tak tum Rey ke rocket ke chief engineer the.”

“Woh alag department tha,” Chotu ne kaha.

Woh uske chehre ko dekh rahi thi. Pichhli mulaqaton mein usne uski awaaz ke achanak rukne ko notice kiya tha. Aaj woh dekhna chahti thi ki sach mein koi farq hai bhi ya woh apni imagination ko zyada importance de rahi hai.

“Ek baat poochhun?” usne seedha kaha.

“Ji.”

“Tum meri taraf dekhte hi kabhi-kabhi itne serious kyun ho jaate ho?”

Chotu ke haath mein phone tha. Usne screen lock kar diya. “Main serious ho jaata hoon?”

“Abhi ho gaye.”

“Didi, aapko sab pata chal jaata hai.”

“Sab nahi. Isliye toh poochh rahi hoon.”

Ek second ke liye kamre mein refrigerator ki halki awaaz hi thi.

Chotu ne nazar uthayi. “Aap... achchi lagti ho. Matlab, aapse baat karna achcha lagta hai. Aur aap office mein bhi itni confident rehti ho. Main kabhi kuch ulta na bol doon, bas.”

Ankita ko jawab ki itni seedhi shakal ki ummeed nahi thi. Usne kaan ke paas se nikal aayi baalon ki lat theek ki.

“Chotu, compliment dena koi exam nahi hai.”

“Achha. Toh pass ho gaya?”

“Conditional pass. Agli baar sentence poora bolna.”

Dono hanse, aur tension ka ek hissa khul gaya.

Ankita ne form ka final document upload karaya. Confirmation screen aayi. Chotu ne khushi se phone utha liya.

“Didi, ho gaya!”

“Ab store ki extra income se meri consulting fee dena.”

“Rey ke liye chocolate aur aapke liye coffee?”

“Coffee chalegi.”

Woh jaane laga toh Ankita ne mehsoos kiya ki uska experiment uske plan se alag nikla. Usne Chotu ki awkwardness ko test karna chaha tha; jawab mein use ek uncomplicated, respectful confession mila. Koi dramatic gesture nahi, koi asaan conclusion nahi.

Door band hone ke baad woh chair ke paas ruki aur stole utha liya. Usne pehna nahi—sirf neatly fold karke shelf par rakha.

Phone par laundry service ka notification chamka: *We have escalated the missing-items case.*

Kal tak is message par uska saara dhyaan hota. Aaj usne use padhaa, phir phone rakh diya.

“Conditional pass,” woh khud se dohrakar muskurayi.

### SHIVANI — TUESDAY, 7:08 PM · SINHA APARTMENT

Shivani ne bell suni toh ek pal ke liye aankhen band ki. Dining table par Chotu ke liye printouts, ek course fee estimate aur do highlighted pages rakhe the. Sab kuch methodically arranged tha.

Woh cream saree aur matching blouse mein thi. Baal bun mein bandhe the. Blouse ki back-dori, jiske baare mein Aditya ne kal yaad dilaya tha, usne do baar check ki. Main fastening secure thi, lekin ek decorative string ka edge ghis chuka tha.

*Tailor ko pehle de dena chahiye tha,* usne socha.

“Good evening, didi,” Chotu ne kaha. Haath mein courier packet aur receipt thi.

“Good evening. Andar aao. Maine institute options compare kiye hain.”

Usne papers table par rakhe. Chotu ne notebook khola. Shivani ne usse fees, duration aur exam dates samjhaaye. Woh har teesri line par question poochh raha tha.

“Didi, entrance interview bhi hoga?”

“Haan, lekin tumhare kaam ka experience useful rahega.”

“Aap mere liye itna time nikaal rahi ho…”

“Main sirf options dikha rahi hoon. Admission tumhe lena hai.”

Woh printer ke paas dusra brochure lene gayi. Usse mehsoos hua ki dori phir loose ho rahi hai. Usne peeche haath badhaya, lekin knot tak pahunchna mushkil tha.

“Chotu, ek help karoge? Yeh decorative dori ka knot khul raha hai. Bas isse seedha kar dena.”

Chotu wahin ruk gaya. “Main? Didi, aap sure ho?”

“Haan. Main pallu hold kar rahi hoon. Sirf knot hai.”

Woh thoda peeche aaya. Shivani ne mirror mein uska hesitant reflection dekha. Usne apne aap ko yaad dilaya ki help maangna uska faisla tha; Chotu ko uncomfortable lag raha ho toh use mana karne ki poori jagah thi.

“Rahne do, agar awkward lag raha hai,” usne kaha.

“Nahi didi, bas dheere se.”

Usne dono dori ends ko align kiya. Knot banate waqt uska haath blouse ke fabric ke paas se guzra. Shivani seedhi khadi rahi. Woh apni heartbeat ko lekar conscious thi, lekin usse bhi zyada Chotu ki careful concentration ko lekar.

“Ek minute,” Chotu ne kaha. “Yeh string pehle se—”

Halki si *tik* ki awaaz aayi.

Decorative dori toot gayi.

Dono ek saath peeche hate. Shivani ne turant pallu sambhala; blouse ka main fastening theek tha. Chotu ke chehre ka rang udd gaya.

“Sorry, didi! Main... maine zyada kheench diya? Sorry, sorry!”

Shivani ne tooti dori haath mein dekhi. Edge puraana aur frayed tha. Use yaad aaya ki kal Aditya ne bhi warning di thi.

“Chotu, suno. Yeh pehle se weak thi.”

“Lekin mere haath se—”

“Accident tha. Main khud repair karwaane wali thi.”

Woh uske saamne khada bhi nahi reh paa raha tha. “Main chalta hoon, didi. Papers dekh liye. Maaf karna.”

“Arre, admission date toh note kar lo.”

“Photo le li. Good night!”

Darwaaza band ho gaya.

Shivani kuch der wahin khadi rahi. Uska pehla impulse khud par gussa karne ka tha. *Maine woh blouse kyun choose kiya? Usse help kyun maangi?* Phir usne dori ko dekha aur seedha conclusion nikala: kapda weak tha, ek awkward moment hua, aur Chotu ne foran distance bana liya.

Woh mirror ke saamne gayi. Uski aankhon mein ab bhi sharm thi, lekin saath hi ek naya respect bhi tha. Chotu ne apology mein kuch zyada bol diya, par usne situation ko exploit nahi kiya.

Usne phone par message likha: *Dori ki fikr mat karo. Pehle se kharaab thi. Aur entrance application Sunday tak bharna. Good night.*

Kuch der baad reply aaya: *Thank you didi. Sorry again.*

Shivani hans padi. *Ek aur sorry aur main ise invoice bhej dungi.*

### SHREYA — TUESDAY, 10:05 AM · MEHTA BOUTIQUE

Boutique ka back corner aaj chhota photo studio bana hua tha. White backdrop, ek ring light, side mein neatly stacked fabric samples. Assistant customer measurements lekar front counter par thi.

“Chotu Babu, courier ke saath photographer bhi banoge?” Shreya ne poochha.

Chotu ne receipt rakhi. “Didi, main toh phone se seedhi photo bhi tedhi nikaalta hoon.”

“Perfect. Aaj experimental art karenge.”

Shreya wine-coloured saree aur sample blouse mein thi. Pallu ka side drape neckline aur waistline ki tailoring dikhata tha—exactly woh detail jo woh prospective clients ke liye highlight karna chahti thi.

“Pehla shot yahan se. Poora outfit frame mein hona chahiye,” usne kaha.

Chotu ne camera khola. “Didi, light peeche se aa rahi hai. Aap thoda left?”

Shreya ne surprise se dekha. “Arre wah. Photography seekh li?”

“Reels dekh-dekhkar.”

Usne phone use diya. “Ab tum ek photo lo. Main do drapes compare karungi.”

Pehle pose mein Shreya ne pallu traditional style mein rakha. Chotu ne shot liya. Doosre mein usne pallu ko shoulder par narrow fold diya, taaki blouse ki shape aur waist-detail clearly samajh aaye. Camera ki screen par result dikhne laga.

“Kaunsa better?” Shreya ne poochha.

Chotu screen ko dekhkar chup ho gaya.

Shreya ko uski wahi purani pause yaad aayi. Is baar usne sawaal dobara calmly poochha. “Fashion wali nazar se batao. Window poster banega.”

“Second,” Chotu ne kaha. “Usmein design ki line clear aa rahi hai.”

“Good. Kyun?”

“First mein pallu fabric ka kaam chhupa raha hai. Second mein poori design samajh aa rahi hai.”

Shreya ko sach mein surprise hua. “Tumne toh correct answer de diya!”

“Didi, aapne hi toh bataya tha ki customers ko product clearly dikhna chahiye.”

“Main tumhe do din mein business consultant bana dungi.”

“Phir aap mujhe payment bhi karna!”

Dono hans pade.

Shreya ne camera screen par apna photo dekha. Usse mehsoos hua ki woh Chotu se ek uncomplicated compliment expect kar rahi thi. Lekin usne photograph ko kaam ke nazariye se dekha tha. Ek chhota disappointment aaya—phir relief.

“Waise,” Chotu ne photo scroll karte hue kaha, “aap pehno toh outfit aur bhi achcha lag raha hai.”

Shreya ne uski taraf dekha.

“Achha? Ab tum fashion judgement se personal judgement par aa gaye?”

Chotu muskuraya. “Dono important hain na, didi?”

Shreya ke gaalon par halka rang aa gaya. “Bahut tez ho gaye ho.”

Tabhi front counter se assistant ne bulaya: “Madam, client aa gaye!”

“Main aayi!” Shreya ne jawab diya.

Chotu ne phone wapas diya. “Main receipt leke chalta hoon.”

“Photos bhejkar jaana.”

“Ji.”

Woh jaane laga toh Shreya ne ek baar pukaara. “Chotu?”

“Ji?”

“Photo genuinely achchi aayi hai. Thank you.”

Dukaan ki ghanti baji. Shreya client ke paas chali gayi.

Baad mein usne pictures dekhi. Second pose chuna. Phir bina zaroorat third photo khol li—jis mein woh shot ke beech hans padi thi. Woh technically imperfect thi, lekin uski smile bilkul asli lag rahi thi.

Phone par Aarav ka message aaya: *Next chord Sunday?*

Shreya ne type kiya: *Haan. Lekin is baar main galat note nahi bajaaungi.*

### TANYA — TUESDAY, 2:03 PM · ARORA APARTMENT

“Finally!” Tanya ne darwaaza kholte hi kaha. “Mister Honest Delivery Boy, tum teen minute late ho.”

Chotu ne wristwatch dekhi. “Didi, do minute.”

“Marketing mein rounding up allowed hai.”

“Accha. Toh aap bhi kabhi late delivery ko maaf kar diya karo.”

“Bilkul nahi.”

Tanya ne use andar bulaya. Table par props sheets ka bundle tha. Laptop par do photographs khuli hui thi. Rust-red cotton-silk saree ke drape ko usne khud style kiya tha; blouse ka fit aur neatly arranged pleats uski usual confident presentation jaisa tha.

“Yeh sheets wahan rakh do,” usne kaha. “Aur ek judgement deni hai.”

“Didi, phir se accountant jaisa test?”

“Nahi. Is baar fashion jury.”

Usne screen uski taraf ghumayi. Pehli photograph front-facing thi: Tanya camera ki taraf seedha dekh rahi thi. Doosri side angle se li gayi thi; saree ki silhouette aur loose hair zyada prominent the.

“Kaunsi choose karun?”

Chotu ne pehle photo A dekhi. Phir B. Phir dono ek saath.

“B,” usne kaha.

“Reason?”

“Usmein aap naturally hans rahi ho. Pehle waali mein lag raha hai kisi ko daantne wali ho.”

Tanya ne aankhein badi kar li. “Oh! Toh main daantti bahut hoon?”

“Main aisa thodi keh raha tha!”

“Ab toh keh diya.”

Chotu hasne laga. “Didi, aap hi toh honest opinion maang rahi thi.”

Tanya ne second photo enlarge ki. “Achha, pose?”

“Achcha.”

“Outfit?”

“Achcha.”

“Sirf achcha? Arjun hota toh poora paragraph bol deta.”

“Phir usi ko bula lo,” Chotu ne bol diya, aur turant chup ho gaya.

Tanya pehle ek second ke liye surprised hui. Phir zor se hans padi.

“Wah! Ab jawab bhi dene lage?”

“Sorry didi.”

“Sorry kyun? Best answer diya tumne!”

Woh chair ke peeche se nikli aur laptop ka lid half-close kar diya. Chotu ne bundle uthaya. Tanya ne uski taraf dekha; woh phir nervous tha, lekin is baar usne pehle se zyada confidently baat ki thi.

“Chotu,” usne kaha, “tumko pata hai in chaaron devaron ki problem kya hai?”

“Sab ek saath bolte hain?”

“Exactly. Tum kam bolte ho, lekin jab bolte ho na, interesting hota hai.”

Chotu ne aankhon mein aankhen daali. “Aap bhi sabko chhedti rehti ho.”

“Correct observation. Full marks.”

Tabhi phone vibrate hua. Laksh ka message tha: *Bhabhi, Chotu interview pass hua kya?*

Tanya ne phone Chotu ko nahi dikhaya. Bas screen lock kar diya.

“Kaun tha?” Chotu ne poochha.

“Koi competitor.”

“Arjun bhai?”

“Competition mein naam nahi batate.”

Dono hans diye.

Chotu receipts sign karaake gaya toh Tanya ne screen par wahi second photo select kar li. Usne realize kiya ki woh uska opinion sirf tease karne ke liye nahi, genuinely jaanne ke liye bhi maang rahi thi.

Laksh ko usne reply kiya: *Interview confidential hai. Result kal.*

Dus second mein message aa gaya: *Matlab pass!*

Tanya muskuraayi. “Tum log kabhi nahi sudhroge.”

### CHIRAG — TUESDAY, 9:20 PM · SOCIETY STORE

Store ka shutter aadha gira hua tha. Bindu Maami ne cashbook band karke ghar jaane se pehle poochha, “Babu, sab deliveries settle?”

“Ji, Maami.”

“Ankita madam ka verification?”

“Ho gaya.”

“Shivani madam ke papers?”

“De diye.”

“Shreya madam ka boutique payment?”

“Done.”

“Tanya madam ke decor sheets?”

“Woh bhi.”

Maami ne muskuraakar kaha, “Aaj toh kamaal kar diya.”

Chotu ne cash drawer close kiya. Kaam ke hisaab se din ordinary tha. Har receipt apni jagah thi; koi pending payment nahi.

Phir bhi uske dimaag mein chaar scenes baar-baar aa rahe the.

Ankita didi ki taraf se aaya woh seedha sawaal—*tum serious kyun ho jaate ho?* Use realise hua ki woh uski nazar aur awkwardness pehle se notice karti rahi thi. Lekin usne use sharminda karne ke bajay mazaak se tension halka ki.

Shivani didi ki dori tootne par uska pehla khayal bas yeh tha ki kahin uski wajah se unhe problem na ho gayi ho. Unka message padhkar rahat mili, lekin usse apni jaldi bhaagne ki aadat par hansi bhi aayi.

Shreya didi ka boutique uske liye ab sirf delivery address nahi raha. Unki camera screen par outfit ke alawa ek confident creator bhi dikhti thi—jo apni art par serious thi, aur phir achanak bachchon ki tarah hans deti thi.

Tanya didi ke saath sabse zyada mushkil tha predict karna ki agla sentence kya hoga. Lekin pehli baar usne bhi joke wapas kiya tha. Aur Tanya sach mein hansi thi.

Usne Maami ki taraf dekha. Woh dukaan ka last switch off kar rahi thi.

“Maami, kal subah koi special delivery hai?”

“Abhi nahi. Kyun?”

“Bas poochh raha tha.”

“Zyada planning mat kiya kar. Padhai ka form bharna hai toh woh bhar.”

Chotu ne sir hila diya.

Phone par Shivani ka reminder tha: *Sunday last date.* Ankita ne bhi documents ki photo bheji thi. Shreya ne selected campaign image forward ki thi: *Thanks, photographer.* Tanya ki ek missed call thi—shayad sheets ka receipt.

Chotu ek-ek message dekhkar muskuraya. Uske andar attraction aur appreciation dono the. Lekin use ab samajh aane laga tha ki kisi ko pasand karna aur uske intentions ko jaan lena alag baat hai.

Usne phone pocket mein rakha aur shutter neeche gira diya.

*“Kal phir wahi chaar ghar honge,”* usne socha.

Phir khud hi jawab diya: *“Lekin shayad main pehle jaisa nahi rahunga.”*

### EPISODE 01 — FINAL CROSS-CUT

Bansal apartment mein Ankita ne verified form ka confirmation screenshot ek folder mein save kiya. Missing laundry set ki enquiry abhi khuli thi.

Sinha apartment mein Shivani ne broken decorative dori ek envelope mein rakhi, tailoring ke liye. Uske course notes bhi table par the.

Mehta home mein Shreya ne campaign ke liye second photograph approve ki aur Sunday ke guitar lesson ko calendar mein mark kar diya.

Arora home mein Tanya ne Laksh ka latest joke ignore karte hue album ke liye selected pictures final kar di.

Chaar homes. Chaar ordinary evenings. Ek delivery boy, jo kal bhi unke darwaaze par aayega.

Aur kuch aise sawaal, jinke jawaab abhi kisi ke paas nahi the.
