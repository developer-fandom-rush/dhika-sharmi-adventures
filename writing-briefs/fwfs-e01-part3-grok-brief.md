## MANDATORY SHAPE FOR EACH MINI-SCENE

1. OBJECT AND ACCESS: Identify who is there, why the elder can enter the location, and which personal clothing item he encounters. The visit must have a credible errand; do not give all four men the same laundry mistake.
2. IMAGINATION: A brief private, inappropriate thought linked to the established hidden attraction; separate thoughts from things that occur in reality.
3. ACTION + REACTION: An unauthorized pause/handling of private clothing and a distinct, visibly unsettled reaction, described non-graphically. This is a breach of trust, not an encounter with the woman.
4. INTERRUPTION + CONCEALMENT: Someone calls or approaches; he restores/puts away the item and tries to appear ordinary. End before discovery by the woman.
