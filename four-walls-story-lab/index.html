Ankita is quiet with strangers but warm and playful with those she trusts. Shivani guards her feelings, Shreya wears her confidence openly, and Tanya notices what others leave unsaid.

This will be a long, lively, slow-burn adult ensemble story. Behind loving marriages, close friendships, family routines and unexpected encounters, curiosity, temptation and sexual tension will build little by little — through meaningful conversations, conflicting emotions, hidden intentions and choices with real consequences. Nothing important happens overnight. Every small moment matters, and every secret brings a new question.
