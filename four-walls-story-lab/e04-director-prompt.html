How each episode moves forward

01 · READ

Verify DHIKA source

240 lines, 25 original-order blocks, exact labels and timestamps.

02 · MAP

Assign counterparts

Only approved FWFS cast, roles, adult-status and established relationships.

03 · CALENDAR

Lock scene day

Keep Friday/Saturday conflict visible; do not silently move events.

04 · SPLIT

Divide parts and scenes

One place, one objective, one output boundary per scene.

05 · DIRECTOR

Approve one scene

Exact raw events kept separately, with approved beat order.

06 · HANDOFF

Send Grok one scene

Target 600–800 words; split rather than omit beats.

07 · AUDIT

Check Grok draft

Compare source, omissions, cast, geography and timeline.

08 · SAVE

Approve and publish

Update reader, calendar, version and status after approval.
