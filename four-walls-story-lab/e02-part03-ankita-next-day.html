Office, flirting and the private favour

L79–109

079Ankita ne agle din, Thursday ko, office mein apne kaam par dhyan lagaya hua tha. Lagbhag dopahar ke 12 baje woh apni desk par kaam kar rahi thi jab ek familiar awaaz sunai di.

080Karan: "Hi Ankita...... aaj bahut beautiful lag rahi ho." Usne cream-green saree aur bright-coloured blouse pehna hua tha.

081Bina nazar uthaye hi Ankita pehchaan gayi ki kaun hai.

082Ankita: giggling.... "Karan.... shhuu.... itni loud nahi. At least baaki colleagues ke saamne mujhe embarrass mat karo."

083Karan: "Hehe.... looking very very beautiful, Ankita," usne uske paas aakar dheere se kaha.

084Ankita: "Hehehe...... enough, stop it."

085Karan: "Ankita, hum tumse ek favour maangna chahte hain."

086Ankita ko pehle studio review ka woh incident yaad aaya jab demo stand hil gaya tha aur Aman ne use girne se sambhala tha. Karan wahan equipment pakad raha tha. Ankita ko uski hidden intention ka pata nahi tha.

087Ankita: "Hehe.... phir koi stand sambhalne ki help chahiye, Aman?" usne teasing tone mein poochha.

088Aman: "Ha.... uh.... nahi Ankita...." Woh startled tha. Use Ankita ke directly address karne ki expectation nahi thi. Woh ek pal ko uske outfit ki taraf dekhkar phir nazar hata gaya.

089Karan: "Ankita..... tum jaanti ho hum dono ko design aur product ideas kitne pasand hain..... but hum office work ke alawa apne ek private project par bhi kaam kar rahe hain."

090Ankita: Puzzled..... "Kaisa project, Karan?"

091Karan: "Yeh humara independent after-office freelance idea hai, jo future mein aane wali ek cheez ke liye hai. Hum isse seriously complete karna chahte hain."

092Ankita: Amazed.... "Wow Karan aur Aman ... future ki itni planning already.... main kaise help kar sakti hoon?"

093Karan ne pehle se socha tha ki project par Ankita ke saath aur time mil sakega. Aman bhi help maangna chahta tha, lekin uska reserved nature Karan se alag tha. Ankita dono ko pasand karti thi; khas taur par Aman's hesitation ko woh kabhi-kabhi halki teasing se ease karti thi.

094Aman: "Errr.. kya tum humein ismein guide kar sakti ho, Ankita?"

095Ankita: looked more puzzled. Kya woh office mein already saath kaam nahi karte... phir yeh alag help kis tarah ki hogi? usne socha.

096Karan: giving Aman a 'you are stupid' look.... "Ankita, uska matlab hai ki office ke baad humare private freelance project mein thodi UX guidance de sakogi? Yeh company ka kaam nahi hai."

097Ankita: hehe... usne Aman ko dekha jo apna sar scratch kar raha tha.

098Ankita Karan aur Aman ko polite, capable colleagues maanti thi. Unke future project ke baare mein jaan kar woh khush hui aur sach mein help karna chahti thi.

099Ankita: "I would be very happy to help my two favourite colleagues."

100Aman: giggling nervously..... "Kab se, Ankita?" usne seedha poochha.

101Ankita: (abhi timing ke baare mein socha nahi tha)..... "Tum log batao?" Woh weekend par extra kaam nahi karna chahti thi, lekin abhi kaha nahi.

102Karan: "Ankita..... hum dono office ke baad haftay mein teen din ek ghanta de sakte hain. Baaki time regular company work aur apni responsibilities rahengi."

103Ankita: "3 days a week !!!!!!"..... usne socha. Phir socha ki agar unhe meri expertise chahiye toh main comfortable evenings mein help kar sakti hoon. Rey aur ghar ki routine ko bhi dhyan mein rakhna hoga.

104Karan: "Ankita....."

105Ankita: "Haan Karan.... okay sure. Office ke baad alternate weekdays mein ek ghanta kar sakte hain. Monday se start karte hain, evening 7 to 8? Meri convenience ke hisaab se confirm kar lenge; kabhi possible na ho toh main mana ya reschedule kar sakti hoon."

106Karan: "Yes Ankita.... thank you... kya aaj hum tumhare saath chal kar apartment dekh sakte hain? Humein pata nahi tum kahan rehti ho."

107Ankita: usne thoda socha, phir kaha, "Okay sure.... main tumhe apartment dikha deti hoon, taaki Monday se aane mein problem na ho. Sirf short visit rahegi."

108Karan: "Okay Ankita.... thank you. Office ke baad milte hain." Dono ne thanks kaha aur apne kaam ki taraf chale gaye.

109Tab Ankita ko yaad aaya ki Ritvik ko naye after-hours project ka plan abhi nahi bataya. Usne call kiya. Ritvik dono colleagues ko pehle se jaanta tha aur bola ki yeh Ankita ka decision hai; use koi problem nahi. Kuch der baat karke call khatam hua.

Shop, apartment and accidental incident

L110–135

110Office ke baad Ankita apni scooty par nikli aur Karan aur Aman apni bike par peeche aaye. Apartment ke paas woh kuch groceries lene aur Chotu ke baare mein poochhne ke liye shop par ruki.

111Ankita: "Please yahin kuch minutes wait karna, Karan..... main groceries lekar aati hoon." Woh dono ko batakar andar gayi.

112Usne kuch items liye aur bill pay kiya.

113Ankita: "Shop owner ji... kya Chotu ye items deliver kar dega?"

114Shop owner: "Chotu leave par hai, Ankita...... usne achanak chhutti li hai."

115Ankita: "Kya woh pichhli baar ki awkwardness ki wajah se leave par gaya hai?" usne socha. Phir boli, "Theek hai, main khud le jaati hoon... thanks." Woh shop se nikal aayi.

116Ankita groceries scooty ke aage rakhkar apartment pahunchi. Karan aur Aman bike par peeche the. Parking mein utarne par dono ne bags uthane ki offer di, lekin Ankita ne mana kar diya. Chotu delivery worker hai, uska kaam alag hai; apne colleagues ko woh unnecessarily trouble nahi karna chahti thi.

117Woh bags khud lekar aage badhi. Handbag ek shoulder par, grocery bag ek haath mein aur file bag doosre haath mein tha. Woh apartment ke darwaze tak aa gaye. Ghar mein filhaal saamne koi nahi dikha; Ankita entrance par apni cheezein sambhaal rahi thi.

118Ankita: "Aman... kya tum please door khol doge... key mere handbag mein hai."

119Aman uske side mein paas khada hua aur handbag ke andar key dhoondhne laga.

120Aman: "Gosh..... usse baby-powder jaisi halki fragrance aa rahi hai," usne mann mein socha.

121Usne kuch nahi kaha, key nikali aur door khola. Ankita ne Karan aur Aman ko pehle andar jaane diya. Woh hall mein khade hokar patiently uska wait karne lage.

122Kitchen ki taraf jaate hue Ankita ne grocery bag neeche rakha aur palatkar unhe dekha. File bag uske haath mein aur handbag shoulder par abhi bhi tha. Woh unki taraf mudi hi thi ki bag slip hokar gir gaya.

123Sirf bag hi nahi gira...... uske shoulder se saree ka pallu bhi saath khinch kar neeche aa gaya. Bag floor par gira aur pallu neeche latak gaya.

124Ek pal ke liye bright-coloured blouse ke upar se uske breasts ka lagbhag ek-tihai hissa nazar aa gaya, jab woh bag uthane ko jhuki. Yeh sudden, accidental exposure tha.

125Woh horrified thi aur samajh nahi paa rahi thi kya kare. Nervousness mein uska free hand pehle bag ki taraf gaya, pallu ki taraf nahi. Jhukne se drape aur neeche hua; kuch seconds baad usne pallu uthakar theek kiya.

126Ankita bahut embarrassed hui aur apne adult colleagues ki taraf dekha. Aman wide-eyed tha aur nazar hatane mein ek pal late hua; Karan awkward mehsoos karke apni nazar hata chuka tha. Ankita kuch keh nahi paayi, excuse karke bedroom mein gayi aur darwaza band kar diya.

127Dono wahin khade the, samajh nahi aa raha tha kya karein.

128Ankita: "Ayyooooo.... main pehle pallu kyun nahi sambhaal paayi... unhone dekh liya..." Woh rone ke kareeb thi.

129Andar se use dono ki halki awaaz sunai di. Kapde aur chehra theek karke woh bedroom se bahar aayi.

130Karan: "Ankita.... kaafi late ho raha hai... hum chalte hain. Monday se aayenge, agar tum comfortable ho." Usne ek saans mein baat keh di.

131Ankita: Use Aman dikha nahi...... "Aman kahan hai?"

132Karan: sar scratch karte hue.... "Woh bike ready karne gaya hai." Karan yeh nahi keh saka ki Aman awkwardness mein jaldi bahar nikal gaya tha.

133Ankita ko uski baat poori tarah believable nahi lagi, lekin usne poochha nahi. Karan ne jaldi se bye kaha aur nikal gaya. Ankita ne door band kiya.

134Ankita: "Oh god..... unhone dekh liya... agar kisi ko bata diya toh? Nahi, dono achhe colleagues hain. Aman shayad embarrassed hokar chala gaya aur Karan mujhe trouble nahi karega," usne socha.

135Poori evening woh sochti rahi ki next time unke saamne kaise aayegi. Woh Ritvik se baat karna chahti thi. Use bahut embarrassment ho rahi thi aur chinta thi ki Ritvik kya kahega.

Ritvik conversation and next-morning hook

L136–150

136Later that night.....

137Ankita: "Ritvik, mujhe tumhe kuch batana hai....." Woh nervous thi.

138Ritvik: use dekhte hue.... "Kya hua? Bolo."

139Ankita: "Aaj ek bahut embarrassing incident hua....." usne dheere se kaha.

140Ritvik: worried.... "Kya hua.... tum gir gayi thi kya, kisi ke saamne?"

141Ankita: "Kaash sirf wahi hua hota....." Phir usne colleagues ke jaldi nikalne tak poori baat batayi.

142Ankita: "Unhone meri chest dekh li..... bahut embarrassment ho rahi hai... ab unka saamna kaise karungi?"

143Ritvik ne kuch pal use dekha. Ankita ko laga woh naraaz hoga, lekin woh achanak hans pada.

144Ritvik: "Hehehehe..... tum dono colleagues ko hi dara kar bhej aayi..." Woh hasta raha.

145Ankita: us par naraaz hote hue.... "Stop it Ritvik.... ab main kya karungi... hansna band karo." Usne lightly uske arm par haath mara.

146Ritvik: "Okay okay.. hehe... kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi... woh accident tha. Dono awkward ho gaye aur chale gaye.... hehe."

147Ankita: "Chiiii..... tum bhi na... main itni embarrassed hoon, aur tum mazaak kar rahe ho."

148Ritvik: "Relax. It was just an accident. Mujhe yakeen hai woh tumhe embarrass nahi karenge aur eventually baat normal ho jaayegi."

149Ritvik ne un dono adult colleagues ke saamne hua embarrassing accident imagine karke ek ajeeb, fleeting mixed reaction mehsoos ki, lekin Ankita ke liye yeh koi mazaak nahi tha. Woh raat bhar pareshan thi aur sochti rahi ki Monday ko unka saamna kaise karegi.

150Agli subah bhi Ankita ko wahi incident yaad aa raha tha. Woh office jaakar unhe face nahi karna chahti thi aur leave lene ka socha. Dhyan hataane ke liye use bas ek hi naam yaad aaya...... Chotu.
