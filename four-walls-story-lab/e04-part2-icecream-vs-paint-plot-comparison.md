### Non-graphic emotional structure that must be protected

1. **Comfort:** Chotu returns willingly; Ankita treats him as welcome company, not just a courier (L044–L052).
2. **Humour:** shared joking keeps the meeting emotionally light; both participate (L053–L060).
3. **Awareness:** ordinary small gestures begin to feel personally significant, without falsely giving either character knowledge of the other's private intentions (L061–L070).
4. **Consequences:** surprise is followed by startled self-awareness, apology and the other person's response, not hand-waved away (L070–L081).
5. **Shared attention:** the narrative alternates viewpoints and records mutual self-questioning about closeness; inner thoughts are distinct from objective facts (L082–L091).
6. **Turning point:** the final quick kiss and immediate response must be treated as a consequential plot event; its next-day evaluation belongs to L099–L100, not the same-day segment (L092–L100).
