LINES 79–109

Meeting & music lessons

Shreya reconnects with Aarav and Neel and makes a practical learning plan.

31 lines

079Saturday subah, Friday raat ke baad Shreya apne routine mein lautna chahti thi. Mahendra se saamna hone ka khayal aate hi usne nashta thoda der se kiya. Samar ne bataya ki Papa ji kisi kaam se bahar nikal chuke hain. Shreya ne rahat ki saans li, lekin Friday ki yaadein poori tarah gayi nahi thi.

080Lagbhag dopahar 12 baje Shreya society ke community hall mein music aur event-equipment ki chhoti meeting ke liye pahunchi. Aaj usne cream-green saree aur bright-coloured blouse choose kiya tha. Wedding rehearsals ke baad yeh Aarav aur Neel ke saath uski pehli aaraam se baithne wali meeting thi.

081“Hi Shreya! Aaj bhi full designer entry,” guitar case kandhe par latkaye Aarav ne khushi se kaha. Shreya ne bina mudkar hi uski awaaz pehchaan li.

082“Aarav, shhh! Meeting hai, award function nahi. Sabke saamne itni tareef mat kiya karo,” Shreya ne hanskar kaha.

083Neel sound-console ki notes band karte hue paas aaya. “Waise aaj ka colour sach mein achchha hai,” usne kaafi dheemi awaaz mein kaha. Aarav hansa, “Dekha? Neel bhi agree kar raha hai.”

084“Bas, bas. Ab dono mera fashion audit band karo,” Shreya ne aankhon se hansi rokne ki koshish ki. Meeting ke baaki log apne-apne instrument aur venue schedule mein busy the.

085“Ek favour maang sakti hoon?” Shreya ne poochha. “Pichhli rehearsal mein maine guitar ka chord bas ek baar try kiya tha. Mujhe sach mein music seekhna hai. Tum dono mujhe sikhaoge?”

086Usse last sound-check yaad aaya—Neel ne permission lekar headset adjust kiya tha aur Aarav ne guitar ke fretboard par uski ungliyon ki placement samjhayi thi. Dono ka approach alag tha. Ab woh practice ko ek casual promise ke aage le jaana chahti thi.

087Shreya ne Neel ko chheda, “Aur is baar mic baar-baar adjust karne ke bahaane mat dhoondhna, sir. Main actual guitar seekhne aaungi.” Neel halka sa jhenp gaya.

088“Arre, guitar mein toh Aarav master hai,” Neel ne jawab diya. “Main tuning, rhythm aur recording mein help kar sakta hoon. Aap seekhna chahti hain toh dono milkar plan bana lenge.”

089Aarav ne guitar case ki taraf ishara kiya. “Aapko chords, strumming aur basic timing se shuru karna hoga. Pehle haath dukh sakte hain, lekin maza aayega.”

090“Wow. Itna systematic plan?” Shreya ne eyebrows uthayi. “Mujhe laga tha tum dono bas guitar pakadkar gaana shuru kar dete ho.”

091Aarav ka outgoing confidence aur Neel ka thoughtful silence use pehle se familiar tha. Dono young adult professionals apne music work ko serious lete the. Shreya ko unke saath seekhne mein genuine interest tha, chahe kabhi-kabhi baat halka sa teasing mood le leti thi.

092Neel ne practical sawaal kiya. “Agar aap comfortable hain, toh hum pehle instrument ka size aur practice space check kar lein? Guitar aur stand kahaan rakhenge, woh bhi dekhna hoga.”

093Shreya ko ek pal ke liye laga ki woh sirf music meeting mein aayi hai aur ab apne ghar par classes plan kar rahi hai. Phir use Aarav ka pichhli baar ka sawaal yaad aaya: 'Next time kaunsa chord seekhenge?'

094“Matlab tum dono mujhe proper homework bhi doge?” Shreya ne kaha. Aarav ne Neel ki taraf dekha. “Bilkul. Aur late homework par fine—ek aur practice session!”

095Shreya hans padi. Neel ne apna sar halkasa khujaya aur bola, “Aarav mazaak kar raha hai. Lekin roz paanch-das minute practice karein toh achchha result milta hai.”

096Shreya ne un dono ke saamne baithkar ek chhota sa beginner plan banaya. Use hobby chahiye thi, koi stage performance ka pressure nahi. Aarav ne basic guitar aur Neel ne tempo aur sound-check ki responsibility lene ko kaha.

097“Mere favourite music teachers tum dono hi ho ab,” Shreya ne mazaak mein kaha. “Lekin fees lene se pehle ek chord toh sahi bajwa dena.”

098“Kab se?” Neel ne seedha sawaal poochha. Aarav bhi dates note karne laga.

099Shreya ko apne boutique, ghar aur Mahi ke routine ka khayal aaya. Weekends bahut unpredictable the; shaam ko ek ghanta nikaalna shayad zyada aasan hoga.

100Aarav ne kaha, “Hum alternate weekdays mein three days, one hour rakh sakte hain. Baki rehearsals aur shows ke saath bhi manage ho jaayega.”

101“Three days a week!” Shreya ne surprise mein kaha. Phir usne socha ki tabhi practice ka flow banega. Woh apne liye koi regular hobby shuru karna chahti thi.

102“Shreya?” Aarav ne pencil rok kar poochha. “Too much lag raha hai kya?”

103“Nahi, okay. Monday se start karte hain—Monday, Wednesday, Friday, seven to eight in the evening. Lekin boutique ka urgent order aa gaya toh pehle inform kar dungi,” Shreya ne clear kiya.

104“Done!” Aarav ne kaha. “Waise aaj ek short visit karke practice corner dekh sakte hain? Monday ko instrument leke aayein toh waqt waste nahi hoga.”

105Shreya ne ek pal socha. “Theek hai, aaj bas setup check. Proper lesson Monday se. Ghar mein main space dikha dungi.”

106Neel ne apni notes fold ki aur Aarav ne guitar case band kiya. “Thank you, Shreya. Meeting ke baad aapki convenience se chalenge,” dono ne kaha. Kisi extra personal plan ki baat nahi hui.

107Tab Shreya ko yaad aaya ki Samar ko music lessons ke plan ke baare mein batana hai. Usne phone kiya. Samar ne kaha, “Tum seekhna chahti ho toh zaroor seekho. Aarav aur Neel ko pehle se jaanta hoon. Bas timing tumhari routine ke hisaab se rakhna.” Shreya ne use setup visit ka bhi bata diya.

108“Dekho, Monday se ghar mein bhi concerts honge,” Samar ne joke kiya. Shreya ne kaha, “Pehle meri strings ki awaaz bardasht kar lena.” Dono hase. Phone rakha toh Shreya relaxed thi.

109Meeting khatam hone ke baad Shreya apni scooty se Mehta building ki taraf nikli. Aarav guitar case lekar apni bike par tha; Neel apna chhota tuner aur notes bag mein sambhaale uske saath aaya. Teeno ne route pehle se confirm kar liya tha.

LINES 110–135

Short home visit

An instrument-space check, an accident and an awkward departure.

26 lines

110Building ke gate par Shreya ne kaha, “Bas yahin park kar lo. Hum seedhe ghar chalte hain. Aaj practice nahi, sirf corner aur instrument stand dekhna hai.”

111Lobby se lift ki taraf jaate hue Aarav ne society hall ke acoustics par baat chhedi. Neel ne bola, “Aapke ghar mein jis taraf noise kam ho, wahi guitar practice ke liye theek rahegi.”

112Shreya ko unki practical baatein sunkar achchha laga. Kal ke complicated dinner ke baad yeh ek ordinary, constructive plan tha jisme woh apne interest ke liye waqt nikaal rahi thi.

113“Aap dono ko building security mein visitor entry karwa di hai,” Shreya ne lift mein kaha. “Bas five-ten minutes lagenge.” Aarav ne smile ke saath answer diya, “Ji, madam student.”

114Mahi us waqt society ki supervised art activity mein thi. Samar ne subah hi use drop kiya tha. Ghar mein unke aane ke dauran hall mein koi bachcha maujood nahi tha; Shreya ko art session ke baad use pick karna tha.

115Shreya ne khud kuch saman utha rakha tha: handbag ek shoulder par, music-meeting ki folder wali tote bag, aur paani ki bottle. Aarav ne bag lene ki offer di. “Nahi, tum guitar sambhalo,” Shreya ne mana kar diya.

116Darwaze tak pahunchte-pahunchte handbag ka strap uske shoulder se halka sa sarak gaya. Usne folder wali tote ko sambhaala aur bag theek karne ke liye ruki.

117“Neel, please key nikaal doge? Handbag ke front pocket mein hai,” Shreya ne kaha. “Mere dono haath full hain.”

118Neel side mein aaya aur bina jaldbazi ke pocket se chabi dhoondhne laga. Aarav thoda peeche guitar case pakde khada raha.

119Neel ko Shreya ke paas ek halki, familiar perfume ki khushboo aayi. Usne koi personal comment nahi kiya; sirf chabi nikaalkar uski taraf badha di.

120Darwaza khulne par Aarav aur Neel pehle hall mein aakar ruk gaye. Shreya unke peeche folder aur bags lekar aayi. “Woh corner,” usne window ke paas ki taraf ishara kiya, “shayad guitar stand ke liye theek rahega.”

121Kitchen counter ki taraf badhte hue Shreya ne bottle rakhne ke liye tote ka strap shoulder se utarna chaha. Bag phisal gaya aur floor par girne laga. Usne reflex mein use pakadne ki koshish ki.

122Bag ke strap mein uski saree ke pallu ka border atak gaya. Folder neeche gira aur uske saath pallu bhi apni set ki hui jagah se khinchkar sarak gaya.

123Shreya jhukkar folder uthane lagi toh blouse ke upar se uske bust ka lagbhag ek-tihai hissa kuch lamhon ke liye nazar aa gaya. Yeh bilkul accidental tha; kisi ka irada kapde hatane ya dikhane ka nahi tha.

124Woh buri tarah ghabra gayi. Uska free hand pehle neeche gire papers ki taraf gaya, pallu ki taraf nahi. Jhukte hue pallu aur neeche aaya; phir use ehsaas hua aur usne turant khud ko cover karke drape theek kiya.

125Shreya ne Aarav aur Neel ki taraf dekha. Neel wide-eyed reh gaya tha aur ek pal ko nazar hatane mein late hua. Aarav awkward ho gaya aur usne turant doosri taraf dekhna choose kiya. Shreya kuch bol nahi paayi; seedhe apne bedroom mein chali gayi aur darwaza band kar liya.

126Dono adult musicians hall mein khade ek-dusre ko dekhte reh gaye. Guitar case abhi bhi Aarav ke haath mein tha, aur practice setup ka kaam shuru bhi nahi hua tha.

127“Ayyooo... pehle pallu kyon nahi sambhaala?” Shreya ne khud se kaha. Use embarrassment aur chinta ho rahi thi ki uski ek silly mistake se dono kya sochenge. Woh kuch pal bedroom mein hi rahi.

128Hall se dono ki halki awaaz aayi. Shreya ne mirror mein saree aur blouse phir se check kiya, chehra normal karne ki koshish ki aur bahar aayi.

129“Shreya, kaafi late ho raha hai,” Aarav ne jaldi-jaldi kaha. “Monday se lesson tabhi shuru karenge jab tum comfortable ho. Aaj hum chalte hain.”

130Shreya ki nazar hall mein idhar-udhar gayi. “Neel kahaan gaya?” usne hairani se poochha.

131Aarav ne apne baal sambhalte hue kaha, “Bike ready karne neeche gaya hai.” Usne yeh nahi bataya ki Neel awkwardness se bachne ke liye almost turant bahar nikal gaya tha.

132Shreya ko bahaana poori tarah convincing nahi laga. Phir bhi usne aur sawaal nahi kiya. Aarav ne guitar case uthaya, short goodbye bola aur lift ki taraf chala gaya. Shreya ne darwaza band kar liya.

133“Oh God... unhone dekh liya,” usne socha. “Kahin kisi ko bataya toh? Nahi, dono decent hain. Neel shayad mujhse zyada embarrassed ho gaya, aur Aarav aise matters ko public nahi karega.”

134Poora afternoon Shreya ko samajh nahi aaya ki Monday ko unhe kaise face karegi. Friday ki awkward family memories ke upar yeh nayi embarrassment bhi jud gayi. Woh Samar ko poori baat batana chahti thi, lekin uske aane ka intezaar kar rahi thi.

135Shaam ko Samar hospital ke kaam se lautkar aaya. Shreya ne chai di, par aaj uski usual teasing shuru nahi hui. Samar ne poochha, “Kya hua, aaj guitar ki sound se pehle hi silence practice kar rahi ho?”

LINES 136–149

Samar conversation

Shreya tells Samar what happened and the couple talk it through.

14 lines

136“Samar, mujhe tumhe kuch batana hai,” Shreya ne dheere se kaha. Uski awaaz se hi Samar samajh gaya ki woh kisi baat ko lekar pareshan hai.

137“Kya hua?” Samar ne seedha poochha. “Sab theek hai? Mahi theek hai na?”

138“Aaj ek bahut embarrassing incident ho gaya,” Shreya ne kaha. Samar ne cup rakh diya aur dhyaan se uske saamne baith gaya.

139“Tum gir gayi thi kya? Kisi cheez se chot lagi?” Samar thoda concerned tha. Uska pehla sawaal Shreya ki safety ke baare mein tha.

140“Kaash sirf folder gira hota,” Shreya ne kaha. Phir usne Aarav aur Neel ka short setup visit, tote bag ka slip hona aur unke awkward exit tak poori baat bata di.

141“Mera pallu neeche aa gaya tha aur blouse se chest ka hissa dikh gaya. Mujhe bahut embarrassment ho rahi hai. Monday ko main un dono ka saamna kaise karungi?” Shreya ne apna chehra dono haathon mein chhupa liya.

142Samar ne ek pal use dekha. Shreya ko laga woh serious ho jaayega, lekin kuch samajhkar Samar hansi rok nahi paaya.

143“Hehehe... toh guitar seekhne se pehle tumne dono teachers ki timing hi bigaad di!” Samar hasta raha. “Instrument setup bhi reh gaya?”

144“Stop it, Samar!” Shreya ne uske arm par halka sa thapki di. “Main already itni embarrassed hoon aur tumhein joke mil gaya?”

145“Okay, okay,” Samar ne apni hansi sambhaali. “Accident tha. Bas agle baar bag aur saree alag sambhaal lena. Dono log awkward hokar chale gaye, aur tum ab poori shaam unke baare mein worry kar rahi ho.”

146“Chiii, tum bhi na,” Shreya ne nakli narazgi dikhayi. “Mujhe tumse sympathy chahiye thi, stand-up comedy nahi.”

147Samar ne uska haath pakadkar kaha, “Sympathy bhi hai. Tumne jaanbujhkar kuch nahi kiya. Aarav aur Neel mature hain; shayad Monday tak sab normal ho jaaye. Agar uncomfortable lage toh lesson postpone kar dena.”

148Shreya ko halki rahat mili, lekin Monday ki pehli class ka khayal ab bhi awkward tha. Samar ka mazaak bhi use yaad aa raha tha. Usne kaha, “Kal se bags sambhaalna mujhe seriously seekhna padega.” Samar bola, “Guitar ke saath ek shoulder-strap workshop free karwa deta hoon.”

149Thodi der baad Shreya kuch ghar ka saman lene society ke paas ki shop par nikli. Use Chotu ka bhi khayal aaya—kaafi din se woh nazar nahi aaya tha. Counter par rukkar usne poochha, “Chotu aaj duty par aaya hai kya?”
